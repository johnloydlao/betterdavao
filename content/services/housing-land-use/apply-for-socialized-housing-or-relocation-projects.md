# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects — Davao City

The **Davao City Housing Office (DCHO)**, part of the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO), handles the city's socialized housing and relocation and resettlement programs. The Citizen's Charter lists these services for **relocation and resettlement beneficiaries of the City Government**, including settlers covered by Section 28 of the Urban Development and Housing Act (UDHA) of 1992.

---

## 1) Step 1: Census Tagging and Interview

A census of the households in a community is the basis for deciding who qualifies for relocation and socialized housing.

1. Write a **letter request** for census tagging and interview, and submit it to the CPDO.
2. The Housing Office assigns a census team. The team verifies the site with you, in coordination with the barangay officials, and **interviews each household**.
3. You verify the answers and **sign the Census Form**. The team gives you a duplicate (pink) copy.

There is **no fee**. The charter lists a processing time of about **14 days**, depending on the number of households and the size of the area.

---

## 2) Step 2: Validation

Submit a **letter request** for validation. Census validators check each informal settler family's qualifications for relocation and socialized housing under the UDHA, and the Housing Office sends a **reply letter with a validation report**. No fee, about 14 days. The data is then encoded in the city's Housing Information System.

---

## 3) For Beneficiaries Awarded a Lot (New Matina Sites and Services)

Qualified beneficiaries can buy **New Matina Sites and Services lots**. The Housing Office's Estate Management Division handles the Agreement to Sell or Lease Purchase Agreement, the monthly amortization, the Deed of Sale, and releasing the deed. Documents commonly required:

- Latest 2x2 picture (2 copies)
- Cedula (current year)
- Marriage contract
- Assessor's Certificate of No Landholding
- Latest Barangay Clearance
- Receipts of all lot payments
- Certificate of Full Payment (for the Deed of Sale)
- If the original awardee has died: death certificate and an Extra-Judicial Settlement or Partition with SPA, published for 3 consecutive weeks

Releasing the Deed of Sale carries a **₱500** fee in the charter.

**Utility certification:** beneficiaries can get a Housing Office certification for a **water connection** (Davao City Water District) and for **building and electrical permits** (Davao Light and Power Company).

---

## 4) Land Titling Help

The Housing Office has run orientations on land documentation and titling for beneficiaries of the Urban Land Reform Program. In February 2024, the city created a Housing Appraisal Committee for residents of the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (S.I.R.) project who want land titles in their names.
