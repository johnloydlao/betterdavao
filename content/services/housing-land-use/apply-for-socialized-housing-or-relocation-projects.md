# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects — Davao City

The **Davao City Housing Office (DCHO)** handles the city's socialized housing and relocation and resettlement programs. It is a separate city department: in November 2023 the City Council made it independent of the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO), where it used to be the Housing Division. The Citizen's Charter lists these services for **relocation and resettlement beneficiaries of the City Government**, including settlers covered by Section 28 of the Urban Development and Housing Act (UDHA) of 1992.

---

## Step 1: Census Tagging and Interview

A census of the households in a community is the basis for deciding who qualifies for relocation and socialized housing.

1. Write a **letter request** for census tagging and interview, and submit it to the Davao City Housing Office.
2. The Housing Office assigns a census team. The team verifies the site with you, in coordination with the barangay officials, and **interviews each household**.
3. You verify the answers and **sign the Census Form**. The team gives you a duplicate (pink) copy.

There is **no fee**. The charter lists a processing time of about **14 days**, depending on the number of households and the size of the area.

---

## Step 2: Validation

Submit a **letter request** for validation. Census validators check each informal settler family's qualifications for relocation and socialized housing under the UDHA, and the Housing Office sends a **reply letter with a validation report**. No fee, about 14 days. The data is then encoded in the city's Housing Information System.

---

## If You Were Awarded a Lot (New Matina Sites and Services)

**New Matina Sites and Services lots** are sold to qualified beneficiaries who have already been awarded a lot. The Housing Office's Estate Management Division handles the Agreement to Sell or Lease Purchase Agreement, the monthly amortization, the Deed of Sale, and releasing the deed. Documents commonly required:

- Latest 2x2 picture (2 copies)
- Cedula (Community Tax Certificate), current year
- Marriage contract
- Assessor's Certificate of No Landholding
- Latest Barangay Clearance
- Receipts of all lot payments
- Certificate of Full Payment (for the Deed of Sale)
- If the original awardee has died: death certificate and an Extra-Judicial Settlement or Partition with Special Power of Attorney (SPA), published for 3 consecutive weeks

**Fees in the charter:** **₱500** when you receive the signed Agreement to Sell or Lease Purchase Agreement for notarization, and **₱500** when the Deed of Sale is released.

**Utility certification:** beneficiaries can get a Housing Office certification for a **water connection** (Davao City Water District) and for **building and electrical permits** (Davao Light and Power Company). The certification fee is **₱50**, paid at the City Treasurer's Office, and the charter lists about 3 days to process.

---

## New Housing Projects

The charter and the city's pages do not list a general application form or an eligibility checklist for new socialized housing applicants. Ask the Housing Office about current projects and how to register.

One current project is **Balai Calinan** in Barangay Talomo River. The city broke ground in August 2026, and it is expected to provide around **630 socialized housing units** for public rental under the city's Public Rental Transitory Housing Scheme.

> **As reported, not yet on an official city page:** in a September 2026 advisory reported by SunStar Davao, the DCHO said the Balai Calinan project does **not** require any membership fee or application payment, that no one outside the Housing Office may collect such fees, and that applicants should deal only with the office. It listed an **Affidavit of No Landholding** and a **Certificate of No Landholding** from the City Assessor's Office as initial requirements. Confirm with the Housing Office before you apply.

---

## Land Titling Help

The Housing Office has run orientations on land documentation and titling for beneficiaries of the Urban Land Reform Program. In February 2024, the city created a Housing Appraisal Committee for residents of the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (S.I.R.) project who want land titles in their names.
