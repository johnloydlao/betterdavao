# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — Davao City

When a court orders eviction and demolition, the **Task Force on Relocation and Resettlement (TFRR)** works with the **Davao City Housing Office (DCHO)** to find out who qualifies for relocation. The services are for **residents of Davao City who have been given a court order for eviction and demolition**: informal settlers, and previous owners of foreclosed PAG-IBIG housing.

---

## 1) How It Works

1. **Census tagging.** The court's sheriff sends the Housing Office a request together with the court order. The Housing Office's census team interviews the affected households and sends a **validation report** to the sheriff. No fee, about 14 days.
2. **Dialogue and meeting.** The TFRR sends notices to the sheriff, plaintiffs, defendants and their counsels, and holds a dialogue and meeting.
3. **Certification for relocation.** The TFRR issues a certification that lists the families who are **qualified or disqualified** for relocation. The charter says the sheriff or plaintiff claims this certification. No fee, about 20 days.

The requirements for these steps are the **court order**, the **sheriff's letter request** and the **census tagging and interview report**.

---

## 2) Before a Demolition (Local Housing Board)

Before a demolition and eviction of illegal structures, the Local Housing Board issues a **Certificate of Compliance**. What the city must show depends on the type of demolition.

**Regular demolition**

- A masterlist of underprivileged and homeless beneficiaries, and minutes and attendance of consultation meetings
- Copies of all notices of demolition given to affected families, with acknowledgment receipts
- Proof of the land: certified copy of the title, tax declaration or tax receipts, location plan or vicinity map, and pictures of the area
- A certification that the area is on the list of danger areas and public areas subject to clearing
- The proposed development plan of the property to be cleared
- An affidavit or agreement on negotiated and voluntary dismantling, if any
- A **certification of the availability of a resettlement or relocation site**, and the development plan of that relocation site

**Summary demolition**

- A list of affected families with their address or location
- Sample notices of summary eviction and demolition given to affected families
- The grounds for the summary eviction and demolition (legal opinion of the City Legal Office, under the UDHA, Republic Act 7279)
- A certification that the affected persons are **not** on the list of potential socialized housing beneficiaries under Section 17 of the UDHA
- Other supporting proof for that certification

The summary demolition checklist does not include a relocation-site certification.

---

## 3) Relocation Sites

The city's published pages do not list specific relocation sites. The **New Matina Sites and Services** lots in the charter are for beneficiaries who have already been awarded a lot, not an open relocation option. See [Apply for socialized housing or relocation projects](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-socialized-housing-or-relocation-projects) for current housing projects.

To ask which sites are available for your community, contact the Housing Office or attend the TFRR dialogue if you have been notified.

---

## 4) What to Do If You Receive a Court Order

- Keep your copy of the court order and the notices.
- Join the census interview and **sign the Census Form** so your household is counted. Ask the census team for a copy.
- Attend the TFRR dialogue and meeting.
- The TFRR releases the certification to the sheriff or plaintiff. Ask the Housing Office or the sheriff whether your household was found qualified.
