# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — Davao City

When a court orders eviction and demolition, the **Task Force on Relocation and Resettlement (TFRR)** works with the **Davao City Housing Office (DCHO)** to find out who qualifies for relocation. The services are for **residents of Davao City who have been given a court order for eviction and demolition**: informal settlers, and previous owners of foreclosed PAG-IBIG housing.

---

## 1) How It Works

1. **Census tagging.** The court's sheriff sends the Housing Office a request together with the court order. The Housing Office's census team interviews the affected households and sends a **validation report** to the sheriff. No fee, about 14 days.
2. **Dialogue and meeting.** The TFRR sends notices to the sheriff, plaintiffs, defendants and their counsels, and holds a dialogue and meeting.
3. **Certification for relocation.** The TFRR issues a certification that lists the families who are **qualified or disqualified** for relocation.

The requirements for these steps are the **court order**, the **sheriff's letter request** and the **census tagging and interview report**.

---

## 2) Before a Demolition (Local Housing Board)

For summary or regular demolition and eviction of illegal structures, the Local Housing Board issues a **Certificate of Compliance**. The city must show, among other things:

- A masterlist of underprivileged and homeless families, and minutes and attendance of consultations with them
- Copies of the notices of demolition
- Proof of the land: title, tax declaration, location plan and photos
- A **certification of the availability of a relocation or resettlement site**, and the development plan of that relocation site

---

## 3) Relocation Sites

The city's published pages do not list specific relocation sites. The charter does show that the Housing Office sells **New Matina Sites and Services lots** to qualified beneficiaries. See [Apply for socialized housing or relocation projects](/apply-for-socialized-housing-or-relocation-projects).

To ask which sites are available for your community, write to the Housing Office or attend the TFRR dialogue if you have been notified.

---

## 4) What to Do If You Receive a Court Order

- Keep your copy of the court order and the notices.
- Join the census interview and **sign the Census Form** so your household is counted. Keep your pink copy.
- Attend the TFRR dialogue and meeting.
- Ask the Housing Office for your certification (qualified or disqualified).
