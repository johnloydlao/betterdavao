# Request Zoning, Land Use or Building Permits — Davao City

Three offices are involved: the **City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)** issues the zoning certification and the locational clearance, the **Office of the City Building Official (OCBO)** issues the building permit, and the **City Treasurer's Office** collects the fees.

---

## 1) Zoning Certification (CPDO Zoning Enforcement Division)

A certification based on the approved Zoning Ordinance. It can be requested by the property owner or an authorized representative.

**Documents**

1. Certified true copy of the title (Registry of Deeds)
2. Accomplished application form (CPDO)
3. Subdivision or sketch plan
4. Order of Payment (CPDO)
5. Official Receipt (City Treasurer's Office)

**Steps:** get the application form at the Public Assistance Counter → present the application and requirements to get an Order of Payment → pay the zoning fee at the City Treasurer's Office → receive the certification.

---

## 2) Locational Clearance for a Building (CPDO)

A written authority from the CPDO, required before you apply for a building permit. The documents for a building of **2 storeys or below** include:

- Order of Payment and Official Receipt
- GIS Zoning Map (from the CPDO)
- Special Power of Attorney from the building owner (if someone else is applying)
- **Seven complete sets of building plans**, signed and sealed by a civil engineer or architect
- Certified true copy of the title (or the original for validation)
- Notarized Locational Clearance Application
- Sketch plan with a scaled vicinity map and landmarks
- Bill of materials
- Notarized Deed of Absolute Sale, or a lease or contract to sell, or an authority to construct from the lot owner
- Accomplished Building Permit Application Form

Extra clearances apply if the site is in a **water resource zone** (WRMC resolution), a **floodway mitigation zone** (DENR clearances and flood-protection studies), or an **ancestral domain (CADT) area** (NCIP certification). Buildings of 3 storeys and above have a longer checklist, and Ordinance 0354-20 requires a 3D presentation for high-rise buildings, hotels, malls and buildings over four storeys.

---

## 3) Building Permit (Office of the City Building Official)

A building permit is required before any building or structure is **erected, constructed, altered, repaired, moved, converted or demolished**. The charter lists:

- Notarized application letter
- Notarized Unified Building Permit Form with civil and architectural plans (5 sets), signed and sealed by a licensed civil engineer or architect
- Sanitary/plumbing, electrical, electronics and mechanical permit forms with signed and sealed plans
- **Zoning Locational Clearance** (CPDO)
- **BFP Fire Safety Evaluation Certificate** (Bureau of Fire Protection)
- Certified true copy of the land title, tax declaration and current real property tax receipt
- Owner's valid ID with three specimen signatures; PRC IDs, PTR and TIN of the signing professionals
- Sketch/lot plan with vicinity map and relocation survey, signed by a geodetic engineer
- Project technical specifications, bill of materials and cost estimates, and a seismic analysis
- Photographs of the location

**Conditional requirements:** a DOLE Construction Safety and Health Program for buildings of 3 storeys or more, boring and plate load test results for structures of at least 3 storeys, a DPWH clearance along a national highway, a CAAP clearance for tall structures, and a DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate for environmentally critical projects.

OCBO also issues occupancy, fencing, excavation, scaffolding, demolition and sign permits, among others.
