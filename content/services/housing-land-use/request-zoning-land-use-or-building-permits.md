# Request Zoning, Land Use or Building Permits — Davao City

Three offices are involved: the **City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)** issues the zoning certification and the locational clearance, the **Office of the City Building Official (OCBO)** issues the building permit, and the **City Treasurer's Office (CTO)** collects the fees.

---

## 1) Zoning Certification (CPDO Zoning Enforcement Division)

A certification based on the Integrated Zoning Ordinance (2019-2028). It can be requested by the property owner or an authorized representative.

**Documents**

1. Certified true copy of the title (Registry of Deeds)
2. Accomplished application form (CPDO)
3. Subdivision or sketch plan
4. Order of Payment (CPDO)
5. Official Receipt (City Treasurer's Office)

**Steps:** get the application form at the Public Assistance Counter → present the application and requirements to get an Order of Payment → pay the zoning fee at the City Treasurer's Office (Sangguniang Panlungsod Building) → receive the certification.

**Fee and time:** the charter lists a zoning fee of **₱750 per hectare** and a processing time of about **3 days and 5 hours**.

---

## 2) Locational Clearance for a Building (CPDO)

A written authority from the CPDO, required before you apply for a building permit. The documents for a building of **2 storeys or below** include:

- Order of Payment and Official Receipt
- Geographic Information System (GIS) Zoning Map (from the CPDO)
- Special Power of Attorney (SPA) from the building owner (if someone else is applying)
- **Seven complete sets of building plans**, signed and sealed by a civil engineer or architect
- Certified true copy of the title (or the original for validation)
- Notarized Locational Clearance Application
- Sketch plan with a scaled vicinity map and landmarks
- Bill of materials
- Notarized Deed of Absolute Sale, or a lease or contract to sell, or an authority to construct from the lot owner
- Accomplished Building Permit Application Form

**Extra clearances** apply depending on where the site is:

- **Water resource zone:** a resolution of the Water Resource Management Council (WRMC)
- **Floodway mitigation zone:** for new development, a Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB) clearance from the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), approved flood-protection measures, and a geohazard and structural study by a licensed geologist and structural engineer
- **Landslide mitigation sub-zone:** for new development, a DENR-MGB clearance, approved slope-protection measures, and a geohazard and structural study
- **Certificate of Ancestral Domain Title (CADT) area:** a Certification Precondition from the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), a Barangay Council Resolution of No Objection, a Memorandum of Agreement with the Tribal Council, and the geographic coordinates
- **If applicable:** an approved Traffic Impact Assessment

Buildings of 3 storeys and above have a longer checklist. See the CPDO section of the [Citizen's Charter, Volume I](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CITY-GOVERNMENT-OF-DAVAO_REVISED-CC_VOLUME-I_20240426.pdf), pp. 572-578.

**Fees and time (2 storeys or below):**

- Zoning fee: **1/10 of 1% of the building cost**
- Filing fee: **₱150 per application** for agricultural and residential buildings, **₱300** for commercial and industrial buildings
- Processing time: about **3 days and 3 hours**

The CPDO issues the Order of Payment with the final amount.

## 3) Building Permit (Office of the City Building Official)

A building permit is required before any building or structure is **erected, constructed, altered, repaired, moved, converted or demolished**. The charter lists:

- Notarized application letter
- Notarized Unified Building Permit Form with civil and architectural plans (5 sets), signed and sealed by a licensed civil engineer or architect
- Sanitary/plumbing, electrical, electronics and mechanical permit forms with signed and sealed plans
- **Zoning Locational Clearance** (CPDO)
- **Fire Safety Evaluation Certificate** (Bureau of Fire Protection)
- Certified true copy of the land title, tax declaration and current real property tax receipt
- Owner's valid ID with three specimen signatures; Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) IDs, Professional Tax Receipts (PTR) and Tax Identification Numbers (TIN) of the signing professionals
- Sketch/lot plan with vicinity map and relocation survey, signed by a geodetic engineer
- Project technical specifications, bill of materials and cost estimates, and a seismic analysis
- Photographs of the location

**Conditional requirements**, among others:

- A Construction Safety and Health Program approved by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) for buildings of 3 storeys or more
- Boring and plate load test results for structures of at least 3 storeys
- A Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) clearance for structures along a national highway
- A Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) clearance for tall structures
- A DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) for environmentally critical projects, and a drainage clearance where the ECC requires one
- A Traffic Impact Assessment for major developments such as malls and convention centers
- A **Road Right of Way clearance** from the City Engineer's Office for structures along city, barangay or subdivision roads

**Fees and time:** the charter lists a processing time of about **19 days and 6 hours**. The fee is computed from the National Building Code schedule of fees, based on floor area and type of occupancy. For example, the charter's sample for a 75 sq m residential building (Division A-1) is ₱4.80 per sq m, or a ₱360 building fee. The full schedule is in the [Citizen's Charter, Volume II](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CITY-GOVERNMENT-OF-DAVAO_REVISED-CC_VOLUME-II_20240426.pdf), p. 455 onward.

OCBO also issues occupancy, fencing, excavation, scaffolding, demolition and sign permits, among others.
