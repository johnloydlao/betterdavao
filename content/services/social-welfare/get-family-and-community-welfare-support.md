# Get Family and Community Welfare Support — Davao City

The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** is the city's social welfare arm. It was devolved to the City Government on October 1, 1992 and provides basic social welfare programs for disadvantaged residents.

---

## 1) CSWDO Programs

- **Family Welfare and Community Welfare Programs**
- **Child and Youth Welfare Programs**, including [early childhood care at Child Development Centers](/enroll-children-in-lgu-daycare-or-preschool-programs)
- **Women Welfare Programs**
- **Elderly and Persons with Disability Programs**, see [senior citizen, solo parent and PWD assistance](/apply-for-senior-citizen-solo-parent-or-pwd-assistance)
- **Emergency Assistance Programs**, see [relief goods and cash aid](/receive-relief-goods-or-cash-aid-during-disasters)
- **Capacity building** for children, youth, women, PWDs, families, older persons and communities
- **Self-Employment Assistance** and business management training, see [livelihood and skills programs](/join-livelihood-or-skills-training-programs)
- **Residential facilities** operated by the CSWDO

---

## 2) Support for Families in Crisis

- **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)** for people facing emergencies
- **Psychosocial and sociocultural support**, for example for solo parents
- **Community organizations:** the CSWDO works through organized groups, such as the 224 solo parent groups, to pass on information about its programs

---

## 3) How to Get Help

1. Visit your **CSWDO district office**, or the main office.
2. Explain your situation. A **social worker** assesses your case.
3. Bring your valid ID, a barangay certification, and any documents the social worker asks for.

The social worker will tell you what is needed for each program. The charter lists these CSWDO services:

- **Educational assistance** for in-school children and youth who are in need of special protection or are disadvantaged. Bring a barangay certification, a certificate of enrolment, a student permanent record (college) or statement of account (technical-vocational), and the _Kasabutan_ from the district office. No fee, about 7 days.
- **Death benefit** of up to ₱5,000 for the family of a senior citizen. See [senior citizen, solo parent and PWD assistance](/apply-for-senior-citizen-solo-parent-or-pwd-assistance).
- **Livelihood assistance**, see [livelihood and skills programs](/join-livelihood-or-skills-training-programs).
- **AICS** (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation), needing only a barangay certification of residency.
- **Social Case Study Report, Certificate of Indigency or Eligibility**, and other social welfare documents.
- **Solo Parent ID** and **PWD ID**.
- **Permit to Travel** for minors.
- **Admission to a CSWDO Residential Care Facility.**
