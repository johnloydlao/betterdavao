# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters — Davao City

When floods, fires or other disasters hit, **barangays respond first**. The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** then lists and profiles affected families, and provides food, relief goods and financial assistance.

---

## 1) What Happens After a Disaster

1. **The barangay responds** and helps families move to evacuation centers.
2. **CSWDO social workers master-list and profile** the evacuees and affected families, and validate the damage to their homes.
3. **Relief is distributed.** In the November 2023 flood, affected families received welfare goods, hygiene kits and hot meals from the CSWDO's **Mobile Kitchen**, and hot meals were also served to evacuees in the evacuation centers.
4. **Financial assistance** is released to families whose homes are validated as damaged.

---

## 2) Financial Assistance (AICS)

The CSWDO gives **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)** to families with **totally, partially or slightly damaged** houses. Not everyone who is affected receives it: eligibility is based on the assessment of the CSWDO's social workers. Your **CSWDO district office** processes the assistance.

The CSWDO has also kept a donation drive open after major flood and fire events.

---

## 3) What to Do

- Stay in contact with your **Barangay Hall** and follow evacuation instructions.
- Make sure the barangay or social worker records your family during the master-listing.
- Keep proof of your address and photos of the damage to your home.
- After the disaster, go to your **CSWDO district office** to ask about AICS.

For general AICS, the charter requires only a **barangay certification of residency**. There is **no fee**, and the CSWDO's social workers assess your situation. The city does not publish a fixed AICS amount, so ask your district office.

Residents who need help paying for hospital care can also ask the Mayor's **Lingap para sa Mahirap** program. See [Go to the local hospital](/go-to-the-local-hospital-for-treatment-or-confinement).
