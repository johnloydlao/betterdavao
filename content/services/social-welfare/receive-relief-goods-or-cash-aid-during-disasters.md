# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters — Davao City

When floods, fires or other disasters hit, **barangays respond first**. The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** then lists and profiles affected families, and provides food, relief goods and financial assistance for damaged houses.

---

## 1) What Happens After a Disaster

1. **The barangay responds** and helps families move to evacuation centers.
2. **CSWDO social workers master-list and profile** the evacuees and affected families at the evacuation sites, and validate the damage to their homes.
3. **Relief is distributed.** In the November 2023 flood, affected families received welfare goods, hygiene kits and hot meals from the CSWDO's **Mobile Kitchen**, and hot meals were also served to evacuees in the evacuation centers.
4. **Emergency Shelter Assistance** is released to families whose houses are validated as partially or totally damaged.

---

## 2) Food and Non-Food Relief

The 2022 Citizen's Charter lists **Emergency Relief Assistance (food and non-food items)** for disaster victims at the evacuation center. It has **no requirements**.

---

## 3) Emergency Shelter Assistance (damaged houses)

The 2022 Citizen's Charter lists **Emergency Shelter Assistance**: financial assistance for "victims of natural and human-induced disasters whose houses are totally, and or partially damaged". The city has repeated this in its disaster updates: "Only those with partially and totally damaged structures or houses are eligible for financial assistance" (CSWDO, after the January floods and landslides). Damaged appliances or furniture do not count.

- **Requirements:** a **Barangay Certification**, and for fire victims, a **Fire Certificate from the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)**.
- **Where:** intake happens at the **evacuation site or another designated area**, during the CSWDO's master-listing and damage assessment.
- **Amount:** the city does not publish a fixed amount. The CSWDO's social workers assess each family.

---

## 4) What to Do

- Stay in contact with your **Barangay Hall** and follow evacuation instructions.
- Make sure the barangay or social worker records your family during the master-listing.
- Get a **Barangay Certification**. If your house burned, also get a **Fire Certificate** from the BFP.
- If you were not reached during the master-listing, ask your **CSWDO district office** what to do.

**Other crisis help.** For other emergencies, the CSWDO gives **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)**. The 2024 Citizen's Charter requires only a **barangay certification of residency**. There is **no fee**, and the CSWDO's social workers assess your situation. The city does not publish a fixed AICS amount, so ask your district office.

Residents who need help paying for hospital care can also ask the Office of the City Mayor's **Lingap para sa Mahirap** program, which gives financial and medical assistance to individuals in crisis.
