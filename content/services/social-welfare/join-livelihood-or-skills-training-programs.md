# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs — Davao City

The City Government of Davao offers livelihood and skills programs through the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** and the **Education Benefits System Unit (EBSU)**.

---

## 1) CSWDO Livelihood Programs

The CSWDO lists these among its services:

- **Self-Employment Assistance (SEA) Program**, for all categories
- **Basic Business Management Training (BBMT)**
- **Capacity building** for children, youth, women, PWDs, families, older persons and communities
- **Capability-building programs** for solo parents to improve livelihood opportunities
- **Skills training** under the PWD Welfare Program

The CSWDO's published pages do not list the requirements, schedules or amounts for these programs. Go to your CSWDO district office, or call the main office, to ask about openings.

---

## 2) Technical and Vocational Training Assistance

The EBSU's **Technical and Vocational Skills Training Program** gives qualified students up to **₱15,000** for tuition and school requirements for TESDA-accredited courses. Courses include Carpentry NC II, Masonry NC II, Automotive Servicing NC II, Welding NC II, Bread and Pastry Production NC II, Computer Hardware Servicing NC II and others.

See [Apply for local scholarships](/apply-for-local-scholarships).
