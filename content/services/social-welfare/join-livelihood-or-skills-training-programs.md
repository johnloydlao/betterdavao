# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs — Davao City

The City Government of Davao offers livelihood and skills programs through the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** and the **Educational Benefit System Unit (EBSU)**.

---

## 1) CSWDO Livelihood Programs

The CSWDO lists these among its services:

- **Self-Employment Assistance (SEA) Program**, for all categories
- **Basic Business Management Training (BBMT)**
- **Capacity building** for children, youth, women, PWDs, families, older persons and communities
- **Capability-building programs** for solo parents to improve livelihood opportunities
- **Capacity-building skills training** for PWDs under the CSWDO's PWD Welfare Program

### Livelihood Assistance (capital loan)

The CSWDO provides a **non-collateral, non-interest-bearing capital loan** with a fund recovery feature to eligible clients. The 2024 Citizen's Charter lists **no fee** and about **5 days and 4 hours** of processing in total. The loan amount is not published.

**Requirements** (per the 2024 Citizen's Charter)

1. Barangay certification of residency (1 original, 1 photocopy)
2. Medical certificate from the City Health Office or a private clinic (for food-related projects)
3. Certificate of attendance at the **Basic Business Management Training (BBMT)**, from the CSWDO district office
4. A **feasibility study**, from the CSWDO district office

The BBMT certificate and the feasibility study come from the CSWDO district office itself, so ask there how to attend the training. (The 2022 Citizen's Charter listed only the barangay certification and medical certificate, with the BBMT as a step arranged by the office.)

**Steps:** submit your documents at the CSWDO **district office** for an intake interview, then a home visit and assessment. If you qualify, the office prepares your Livelihood Availment Form and sends the documents for approval, and informs the district office when the assistance is ready for release.

Schedules for the BBMT are not published, so ask your district office.

---

## 2) Technical and Vocational Training Assistance

The EBSU's **Technical and Vocational Assistance Program** gives qualified Davao City residents up to **₱15,000** for tuition and other school requirements when they enroll in a technical-vocational school accredited by TESDA (the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority). No grantee can benefit from more than one EBSU program, and the 2024 Citizen's Charter lists April/June as the acceptance period. Courses include Carpentry NC II, Masonry NC II, Automotive Servicing NC II, Welding NC II, Bread and Pastry Production NC II, Computer Hardware Servicing NC II and others. NC II is a TESDA National Certificate (Level II).

For other city scholarships, see the Education section under Services.
