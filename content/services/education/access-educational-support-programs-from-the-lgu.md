# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Davao City

The City Government of Davao supports learners from early childhood to college through the **Education Benefits System Unit (EBSU)** and the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)**.

---

## 1) Early Childhood (Ages 3–4)

- **Child Development Centers** run by the CSWDO's Early Childhood Care and Development program, with height and weight monitoring and supplementary feeding.
- **Parents Teaching Other Parents (PTOP)** for communities without a nearby center.

See [Enroll children in daycare or preschool programs](/enroll-children-in-lgu-daycare-or-preschool-programs).

---

## 2) College, Technical and Vocational

- **Scholarship on Tertiary Education Program (STEP)** for indigent college students
- **Technical and Vocational Skills Training Program**: up to ₱15,000
- **Other Educational Assistance** for differently-abled persons: up to ₱10,000 a year
- **Financial Assistance for Lumad**: ₱1,500 a month for 10 months
- **Medical and Law School Educational Assistance** for Lumad students

See [Apply for local scholarships](/apply-for-local-scholarships) for qualifications and documents.

---

## 3) Where to Go

- **EBSU:** Room 28, 4th Floor (rooftop), City Hall Building, San Pedro Street. Phone (082) 241-1000 local 247. Email ebsustep@gmail.com.
- **Your barangay's Child Development Center** or Barangay Hall for early childhood programs.

The city's published pages do not list other school-level assistance, such as school supplies or feeding for elementary and high school students. Ask your school or the EBSU about what is available.
