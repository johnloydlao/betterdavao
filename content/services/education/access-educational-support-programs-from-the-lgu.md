# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Davao City

The City Government of Davao supports learners from early childhood to college through the **Educational Benefit System Unit (EBSU)** and the **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)**.

---

## 1) Early Childhood (Ages 3–4)

- **Child Development Centers** run by the CSWDO's Early Childhood Care and Development program, with height and weight monitoring and supplementary feeding. The CSWDO says enrollment is free.
- **Parents Teaching Other Parents (PTOP)** for communities without a nearby center.

See [Enroll children in daycare or preschool programs](/services/education/enroll-children-in-lgu-daycare-or-preschool-programs).

---

## 2) College, Technical and Vocational (EBSU)

- **Scholarship on Tertiary Education Program (STEP)** and **STEP – Financial Assistance (STEP-FA)** for college students
- **Special Educational Assistance Program (SEAP)**: up to ₱10,000 per school year
- **Technical and Vocational Assistance Program**: up to ₱15,000
- **Educational Assistance Program for Persons with Disability**: up to ₱10,000 a year
- **Financial Assistance Program for Lumads**: a ₱1,500 monthly stipend (2024 Citizen's Charter)
- **Medical and Law Education Assistance Program** for Davao City residents admitted to a Davao City-based medical or law school

Applications are made online through the [EBSU eScholar portal](https://ebsu-escholar.davaocity.gov.ph/). See [Apply for local scholarships](/services/education/apply-for-local-scholarships) for amounts, qualifications and documents.

---

## 3) CSWDO Educational Assistance

The CSWDO gives financial educational assistance to eligible in-school children and youth. The charter lists who may avail as "Children in Need of Special Protection (Children at Risk, Children in Conflict with the Law) and Disadvantaged Youth".

Apply at your **CSWDO district office** with:

1. Barangay Certification from your Barangay Hall (1 original, 1 photocopy)
2. Student Permanent Record, for college students (1 original, 1 photocopy)
3. Statement of Account, for technical-vocational and college students (1 original, 1 photocopy)
4. Certificate of Enrolment, for elementary, high school, vocational and college students (1 original, 1 photocopy)
5. The _Kasabutan_ from the CSWDO district office (1 original, 1 photocopy). _Kasabutan_ is Cebuano for "agreement"; the charter does not describe the form further, so ask the district office about it.

There is no fee, and the charter lists about **7 days** of processing. It includes an intake interview, a home visit and an assessment.

---

## 4) Library Services

A library ID card costs ₱30 a year, and schools and barangays can request the Mobile Library. See [supplementary activities for schools](/services/education/learn-about-supplementary-activities-for-schools-in-your-area).

The city's published pages do not list other school-level assistance, such as school supplies. Ask your school or the EBSU what is available.
