# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area — Davao City

The City Government of Davao supports school and early childhood activities mainly through the **CSWDO** and the **City Health Office (CHO)**. The city does not publish one directory of school activities, so this page covers what is documented.

---

## 1) Child Development Centers

- **Bayanihan Bulilit:** before classes open, parents and the community help day care workers clean and improve the Child Development Center (as described by the CSWDO in 2022).
- **Moving-Up Ceremonies:** held at the end of the school year to celebrate young learners' achievements (March 23–27 in 2026).

---

## 2) Health Education in Schools

- The CHO coordinates with schools to bring health personnel on campus and educate parents on immunization during **Parent-Teacher Assemblies** (April 2026).
- **Purok Kalusugan** brings health lectures and campaigns to every purok in the barangays.

---

## 3) Library Programs

The **Davao City Library and Information Center** offers services that schools and barangays can request:

- **Mobile Library Service / Mobile Disaster Education Library:** brings books and reading materials to people who need access to them. **Schools and barangays** can request it with a **letter of request** signed by the head of school or the Barangay Captain and addressed to the City Librarian. The charter lists no fee, and a day to approve or reschedule the request.
- **Library ID card:** an annual membership card for all library users, for **₱30**, at the Main Library or the district libraries (such as Calinan and Toril). Bring a valid ID.
- **Book borrowing:** with a borrower's card and a qualified guarantor.

**Main Library:** 3rd Floor, SP Building, San Pedro St., Davao City. Phone: (082) 222-7845. Fax: (082) 225-3137 (per the 2024 Citizen's Charter directory, which lists "225-3137 (fax)/222-7845").

---

## 4) Finding Activities at Your Child's School

- Ask the school or your child's Child Development Center for its calendar of activities.
- Follow the City Government's news at [davaocity.gov.ph](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/) for citywide programs.
