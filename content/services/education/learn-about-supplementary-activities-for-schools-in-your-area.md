# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area — Davao City

The City Government of Davao supports school and early childhood activities mainly through the **CSWDO** and the **City Health Office (CHO)**. The city does not publish one directory of school activities, so this page covers what is documented.

---

## 1) Child Development Centers

- **Bayanihan Bulilit:** before classes open, parents and the community help day care workers clean and improve the Child Development Center.
- **Moving-Up Ceremonies:** held at the end of the school year to celebrate young learners' achievements (March 23–27 in 2026).
- Workers prepare **activity guides** so children can keep learning at home.

---

## 2) Health Education in Schools

- The CHO coordinates with schools to bring health personnel on campus and educate parents on immunization during **Parent-Teacher Assemblies**.
- **Purok Kalusugan** brings health lectures and campaigns to every purok in the barangays.

---

## 3) Finding Activities at Your Child's School

- Ask the school or your child's Child Development Center for its calendar of activities.
- Follow the City Government's news at [davaocity.gov.ph](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/) for citywide programs.

---

_Sources: City Government of Davao news from the CSWDO and the City Health Office (davaocity.gov.ph)._
