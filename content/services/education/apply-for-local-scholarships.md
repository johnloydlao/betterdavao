# Apply for Local Scholarships — Davao City

The City Government of Davao offers educational assistance through the **Educational Benefit System Unit (EBSU)** under the Office of the City Mayor. You apply online through the EBSU's **[eScholar portal](https://ebsu-escholar.davaocity.gov.ph/)**.

---

## 1) Programs

| Program                                                        | Who it is for                                                             | Benefit                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Scholarship on Tertiary Education Program (STEP)**           | Davao City residents going to college                                     | The charter does not state an amount. Ask the EBSU.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **STEP – Financial Assistance (STEP-FA)**                      | Davao City residents going to college                                     | **SY 2025–2026 amounts:** Category A **₱25,000 per semester** (average of at least 93); Category B **₱20,000 per semester** (90 to 92); Category C **₱7,500 per semester** (80 to 89). Confirm this year's amounts with the EBSU. |
| **Special Educational Assistance Program (SEAP)**              | Davao City residents                                                      | Up to **₱10,000 per school year**, and no more than **₱5,000 per term or semester**                                                                                                                                               |
| **Technical and Vocational Assistance Program**                | Davao City residents enrolling in a TESDA-accredited school               | Up to **₱15,000** for tuition and other school requirements                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Educational Assistance Program for Persons with Disability** | Differently-abled persons or persons with disability                      | Up to **₱10,000 a year** for tuition, transportation and other education-related expenses                                                                                                                                         |
| **Financial Assistance Program for Lumads**                    | Indigent students from an indigenous tribe of Davao City                  | **₱1,500 monthly stipend** (2024 Citizen's Charter). See the note below.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Medical and Law Education Assistance Program**               | Davao City residents admitted to a Davao City-based medical or law school | A full or half scholarship grant plus a book allowance. See the note below.                                                                                                                                                       |

**Medical and Law:** the charter describes a "full/half scholarship grant" covering tuition, miscellaneous and other fees assessed by the school, plus a book allowance "amounting to Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20,000.00) and Ten Thousand Pesos (P10,000.00) respectively" for medical school, and "Eight Thousand Pesos (P8,000.00) and Four Thousand Pesos (P4,000.00) respectively" for law school. It does not say which allowance goes with which grant, so ask the EBSU.

**Lumad stipend:** the archived EBSU program page (2024) says the ₱1,500 stipend is paid for 10 months, for up to 4 academic years, through ATM. City news in April 2025 described **₱7,500** in financial assistance per qualified IP student for A.Y. 2025–2026, without saying how often it is paid. Confirm the current amount with the EBSU.

The archived Technical and Vocational page (2024) says the benefit is paid to the school after you submit the Statement of Account. The charter's Technical and Vocational entry adds that no grantee can benefit from more than one program.

**City College of Davao:** city news in April 2025 reported that the college "offers free tuition to Dabawenyo students", with IP students given priority in admission. Ask the college about admission.

## 2) STEP Qualifications

The 2024 Citizen's Charter lists only "Resident of Davao City". The archived EBSU STEP page (2024) gives these qualifications:

- A **resident of Davao City** with good moral character.
- From an **indigent or below-average income family**, as pre-determined by the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO).
- A graduate of a public or private secondary school in Davao City.
- Enrolling in a course prescribed by STEP, following CHED priority programs, at a CHED-recognized school in Davao City.
- Not receiving another scholarship or financial aid from a government agency, NGO or foundation. An academic scholarship from the school for honor students is allowed.
- **Only one child per family** can avail of the scholarship.

## 3) Documents

For STEP, STEP-FA, SEAP and Technical and Vocational, the 2024 Citizen's Charter lists:

**Initial requirements**

- Report Card or Transcript of Records (1 photocopy), or the ALS Accreditation and Equivalency Test result (1 photocopy).

**Additional requirements after evaluation**

1. Certificate of Residency from your barangay (1 original)
2. Certificate of Good Moral Character from your school (1 photocopy)
3. Certificate of Indigency or Eligibility from the CSWDO (1 original)
4. Income Tax Return of **both parents** if both are working, or a Certificate of Tax Exemption if not working (1 photocopy)
5. 2x2 ID picture (1 piece)

**Other programs**

- **Technical and Vocational:** you must also pass the EBSU's pre-qualifying exam.
- **Lumad:** add a Certificate of Confirmation from the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) (1 original). You must also pass the pre-qualifying exam.
- **Persons with disability:** the initial requirements are a letter request from the parent or guardian, and a beneficiary's profile and letter of confirmation from the foundation or intervention center.
- **Medical and Law:** the charter lists a longer set, including your Transcript of Records, certification of your General Weighted Average, NMAT (medical) or PhilSAT (law) result, a certificate of admission from a Davao City-based medical or law school, a medical certificate from a government hospital, your parents' Statement of Assets, Liabilities and Net Worth where available, and a sworn statement that no parent or sibling is already a city scholar. The eScholar portal lists an NMAT score of at least 60% for medical applicants.

## 4) How to Apply

The charter lists applications as accepted in **April/June**. In 2025, the city announced applications from **April 21 to 30** through eScholar.

1. Create an account on the **[eScholar portal](https://ebsu-escholar.davaocity.gov.ph/)** and follow the posted procedure.
2. Upload your requirements for evaluation.
3. Attend the interview, if you qualify (STEP, Lumad, and Medical and Law).
4. Wait for the results, posted on the City Government of Davao's official Facebook page and the EBSU bulletin board. The charter lists about **14 days**.
5. Attend the orientation for new scholars (STEP, Lumad, and Medical and Law).
6. Enroll in your chosen school. The EBSU endorses qualified applicants to their schools.

There is no fee to apply.

> **As reported, not yet on an official city page:** SunStar Davao reported in April 2026 that applications for A.Y. 2026–2027 ran from **April 6 to 24, 2026** through eScholar, and that priority courses included teacher education (science and mathematics), information technology, health and allied health courses, and social sciences. Confirm with the EBSU office.
