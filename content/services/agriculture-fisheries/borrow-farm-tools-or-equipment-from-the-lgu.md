# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment From the LGU — Davao City

The charter has no tool-lending service for **individual farmers**. Members of **registered farmers' organizations** can borrow **small irrigation pumps** (below), and registered organizations can request machinery and equipment support through the City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO).

---

## 1) Small Pump Irrigation Support

The CAgrO's Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering Division lends small irrigation pumps under the Agricultural and Fisheries Mechanization (AFMech) Law (RA 10601). It is open to **members of a registered farmers' organization**.

**Documents**

1. Endorsement from the Municipal Agricultural Office (your CAgrO district office)
2. Letter of intent from the farmers' organization
3. Organizational profile
4. Borrower's slip (2 originals, from the CAgrO)

**Steps**

1. Go to your district office, sign the logbook at the Public Assistance Desk, and submit the letter of intent and organizational profile.
2. Get a copy of the endorsement. The district office forwards your documents to the CAgrO main office.
3. The CAgrO schedules a **site validation** and checks that your water source is feasible. Sign and get a copy of the validation report.
4. The CAgrO delivers and tests the pump on site and orients your operator on its use and maintenance.
5. Fill out and sign the **borrower's slip** and logbook.

There is no fee. The charter lists a total of about 6 days and 35 minutes.

---

## 2) Agricultural and Fishery Machinery and Equipment Assistance

The same division gives technical assistance on the provision of **agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment**. It is open to a **registered farmers' organization, through the barangay**.

**Documents**

1. Endorsement from the Municipal Agricultural Office
2. Letter of intent from the farmers' organization
3. Barangay resolution
4. Certified true copy of the organization's registration (DOLE, CDA or SEC)
5. Barangay agricultural profile

**Steps:** submit the documents to your district office, which endorses them to the main office. The CAgrO sets a schedule, conducts a **site validation** and confirms the feasibility of the project. It then prepares a project design and technical specifications and submits them to the **Local Finance Committee** for budget approval, and your group is told the funding status. The charter's steps end with this funding request. They do not say when, or whether, equipment is released.

There is no fee. The charter lists a total processing time of 19 days and 35 minutes.

---

## 3) Rainwater Catchment Projects

Open to **Davao City's farmers and fisherfolk**, under City Ordinance No. 0298-09 on rainwater harvesting. It has two stages.

**Validation** (about 6 days and 35 minutes, no fee). Submit to your district office:

1. Endorsement from the Municipal Agricultural Office
2. Barangay council resolution with the list of beneficiaries
3. A request letter or request form for each beneficiary in the barangay

After the site validation, the CAgrO gives you the checklist for the next stage.

**Establishment** (about 19 days and 5 minutes, no fee). The CAgrO builds three rainwater catchment units. Submit:

1. Proof of ownership (land title, photocopy)
2. Notarized Agreement of Usufruct (for individual landowners in alienable and disposable lands)
3. Barangay certification (if you are an heir of the landowner named in the title)
4. Certification Precondition from the NCIP Regional Office (for ancestral domain areas)
5. Notarized Consent to Grant Right of Way (for individual tribal rights owners in ancestral domain areas)

---

## 4) Other Support

- Technical assistance on **agricultural and fishery infrastructure projects**.
- Technical assistance on **accrediting rural-based organizations** (CSOs), which helps groups qualify for support.

---

## 5) Tips

- Organize or join a **registered farmers' association** and get it accredited through your barangay.

> **As reported by the City Information Office:** in [August 2024](https://davaocity.gov.ph/agriculture/cagro-to-farmers-aid-services-available-in-district-offices/) the CAgrO asked farmers to contact their district offices for technical assistance and support, "including farm machineries and pesticides". Confirm with your district office what is available in your area.
