# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment From the LGU — Davao City

The city's published services do not describe a program for **lending farm tools to individual farmers**. Machinery and equipment support goes to **registered farmers' organizations** through the City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO).

---

## 1) Agricultural and Fishery Machinery and Equipment Assistance

The CAgrO's Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering Division provides technical assistance on the provision of **agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment**. It is open to a **registered farmers' organization, endorsed through the barangay**.

**Documents**

1. Endorsement from the Municipal Agricultural Office (your CAgrO district office)
2. Letter of intent from the farmers' organization
3. Barangay resolution
4. Certified true copy of the organization's registration (DOLE, CDA or SEC)
5. Barangay agricultural profile

**Steps:** go to your district office and submit the letter, which is reviewed and endorsed. The CAgrO sets a schedule, conducts a **site validation**, confirms the feasibility of the request and prepares the recommendation. There is no fee. The charter lists about **12 days** for the final step, so expect a few weeks.

---

## 2) Other Equipment Support

- **Small pump irrigation support** and **rainwater catchment projects** are also listed as CAgrO services, with a validation step. Ask the district office for the requirements.
- Technical assistance on **agricultural and fishery infrastructure projects**.
- Technical assistance on **accrediting rural-based organizations** (CSOs), which helps groups qualify for support.

---

## 3) Tips

- Organize or join a **registered farmers' association** and get it accredited through your barangay.
- Ask your district office whether any tools or equipment of the CAgrO are available for use in your area.
