# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers or Fingerlings — Davao City

The **City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO)** distributes planting materials and fingerlings to farmers and fish farmers to raise productivity and food security. The charter lists **no fee** for the distribution.

---

## 1) What You Can Request

- **Seedlings and planting materials:** fruit trees, industrial crops, and tissue-cultured plantlets of banana (lakatan and saba) and abaca
- **Seeds:** vegetables, rice and corn, leguminous crops, root crops
- **Fingerlings:** tilapia and hito, for fish farmers with ponds or inland bodies of water
- **Biological control agents:** _Trichoderma_ and _Trichogramma_ wasps (for Asian corn borer), produced by the CAgrO under the City Organic Agriculture Ordinance No. 0384-10 and distributed to interested farmers

The CAgrO's published services do not list fertilizer distribution. Ask your district office whether any is available.

---

## 2) Who Can Apply

The charter says **all** may apply. Materials go to farmers and qualified individuals **with available land**, and to fish farmers who have ponds or inland bodies of water.

---

## 3) How to Request

1. Go to your **CAgrO district office (Municipal Agricultural Office)**, or the main office, and sign the logbook at the Public Assistance Desk.
2. Submit a **request letter** and fill out the **request form** (given by the CAgrO).
3. The office validates your request (a validation report) and issues a **Request and Issue Slip**.
4. Claim the materials. Ask your district office about stock and schedules.

For biological control agents, the request letter and form are coursed through the district offices.

Related help from the CAgrO: [training and technical assistance](/attend-farming-or-fishing-training), free **crop insurance enrollment** with the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (you must be registered in the RSBSA first), and **RSBSA enrollment** (bring a valid ID, a land title, tax declaration or barangay certification, and a 2x2 picture).
