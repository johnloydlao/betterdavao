# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers or Fingerlings — Davao City

The **City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO)** distributes planting materials and fingerlings to farmers and fish farmers to raise productivity and food security. The charter lists **no fee** for the distribution.

---

## 1) What You Can Request

- **Seedlings and planting materials:** fruit trees, industrial crops, and tissue-cultured plantlets of banana (lakatan and saba) and abaca
- **Seeds:** vegetables, rice and corn, leguminous crops, root crops
- **Fingerlings:** tilapia and hito, for fish farmers with ponds or inland bodies of water
- **Biological control agents:** _Trichogramma_ wasps (against the Asian corn borer and similar pests) and _Trichoderma_, two biological control agents produced by the CAgrO under the City Organic Agriculture Ordinance No. 0384-10 and distributed to interested farmers

The charter does not list a fertilizer service.

> **As reported by the City Information Office:** the CAgrO gave free fertilizer to [200 durian farmers in April 2024](https://davaocity.gov.ph/agriculture/200-durian-farmers-receive-free-fertilizer-from-cagro/) and to [200 cacao farmers in June 2024](https://davaocity.gov.ph/agri-business/davao-city-lgu-provides-free-fertilizers-to-cacao-farmers/), as support for those crops. Ask your district office whether a program covers your crop, and confirm with the office before you go.

---

## 2) Who Can Apply

The charter says **all** may apply. Materials go to farmers and qualified individuals **with available land**, and to fish farmers who have ponds or inland bodies of water.

- **Registry:** the office checks that you are enrolled in the **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)** (farmers) or the **Municipal Fisherfolk Registry System** (fisherfolk). If you are not, it will help you enroll first. See the RSBSA requirements below.
- **Minimum area** (checked during the site validation):
  - Vegetable seeds: 0.05 hectare
  - Corn seeds: 0.5 hectare
  - Palay (rice) seeds: 0.10 hectare
  - Fruit tree seedlings: 0.25 hectare
  - Tissue-cultured plantlets: 0.10 hectare
  - Hito and tilapia fingerlings: 50 square meters

---

## 3) How to Request

1. Go to your **CAgrO district office (Municipal Agricultural Office)**, or the main office, and sign the logbook at the Public Assistance Desk. The main office refers you to the district office where your farm is.
2. Submit a **request letter** and fill out the **request form** (given by the CAgrO). If the materials are out of stock, the office takes your contact details and calls you when they are available.
3. The office validates your site (a validation report) and issues a **Request and Issue Slip**.
4. Claim the materials where the office tells you. For fingerlings, it gives you the dispersal schedule and venue.

The charter lists about 2 days, 1 hour and 20 minutes in total.

For biological control agents, the request letter and form are coursed through the district offices.

Related help from the CAgrO: [training and technical assistance](/services/agriculture-fisheries/attend-farming-or-fishing-training), free help **enrolling in crop insurance** with the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC) (you must be registered in the RSBSA first), and **RSBSA enrollment** (bring a valid ID, a land title, tax declaration or barangay certification, and a 2x2 picture).
