# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — Davao City

The **City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO)** gives trainings and free technical help to farmers and fisherfolk.

---

## 1) Trainings

Trainings cover **crop and fishery production, livelihood, processing, and value-adding** of agri-fishery products, to raise production and income. They are open to **interested groups, farmers and fisherfolk**.

**To request a training:** fill out a request form at the CAgrO, or submit a walk-in request letter. The office also runs techno demos, field exposure and crop production monitoring.

---

## 2) Free Technical Assistance

- **Crop and fishery production:** soil management, seed selection, planting and irrigation methods, pests, fertilizers, harvesting and post-harvest, and field management
- **Soil analysis:** bring a dried soil sample (1 kilo)
- **Water sampling and analysis** for fish ponds
- **Pest and disease management**
- **Crop insurance enrollment** (Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation)
- **RSBSA enrollment** (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)
- **Business plan preparation** and marketing support for agri-fishery enterprises

Request these at your **district office (Municipal Agricultural Office)** or the main office.

---

## 3) For Fisherfolk

The CAgrO issues licenses and permits under the city's Comprehensive Fisheries Ordinance. You pay the fees at the City Treasurer's Office after the CAgrO gives you an order of payment.

**Fishing license** (for registered fisherfolk of Davao City). Fee: **₱50.00**. Bring:

- The accomplished form
- Proof that you are in the CAgrO's Registry of Fisherfolk
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- Your previous fishing license, if you are renewing

**Boat registration certificate** (for motorized or non-motorized boats or bancas of **3 gross tons or less**). Fee: **₱50.00 non-motorized, ₱75.00 motorized**. The CAgrO inspects and measures the boat and checks its color coding. Bring:

- The accomplished registration form
- Proof that you are in the Registry of Fisherfolk
- Certificate of Boat Ownership (from the barangay hall)
- Fishing Vessel Clearance Certificate (from the PNP-Maritime)

**Municipal fisheries license** (for operators of fish cages, fish corrals, shallow fish shelters and seaweed farms in Davao City waters). The CAgrO inspects the site first. Bring:

- The application form, filled out and notarized
- Certificate of No Objection from the Barangay Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Council (BFARMC)
- Barangay resolution interposing no objection
- Certificate of No Objection from the City Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Management Council (CFARMC)

Fees: fish cages ₱1,000.00 per 100 sq.m. or fraction; seaweed farming ₱20.00 per 100 m culture line; fish corrals ₱500.00 per unit; shallow fish shelters ₱200.00 per unit.

**Auxiliary invoice** (fishery permit to transport) for fish and fishery products leaving Davao City. Fees:

- Fresh, frozen, dried or preserved fish and fishery products: ₱200.00 per ton (and fraction)
- Bangus fry or other species: ₱30.00 per thousand
- Bangus fingerlings or other species: ₱50.00 per thousand
- Live fish: ₱50.00 per thousand
- Empty shells of non-prohibited species: the charter gives both ₱200 and ₱20 per ton, so ask the CAgrO

> **As reported by the City Information Office:** the CAgrO holds onsite fishing license and boat registration drives in coastal barangays, joined by the PNP-Maritime, the Philippine Coast Guard, the Department of Agriculture's RSBSA team and the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation ([January 2025](https://davaocity.gov.ph/agriculture/cagro-fishing-license-boat-registration-continues/), [February 2026](https://davaocity.gov.ph/agriculture/davao-city-urges-fisherfolk-to-secure-licenses/)). The February 2026 report gives ₱70.00 for motorized boats, not the charter's ₱75.00. Confirm the fee and the next schedule with the office.
