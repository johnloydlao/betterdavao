# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — Davao City

The **City Agriculturist's Office (CAgrO)** gives trainings and free technical help to farmers and fisherfolk.

---

## 1) Trainings

Trainings cover **crop and fishery production, livelihood, processing, and value-adding** of agri-fishery products, to raise production and income. They are open to **interested groups, farmers and fisherfolk**.

**To request a training:** fill out a request form at the CAgrO, or submit a walk-in request letter. The office also runs techno demos, field exposure and crop production monitoring.

---

## 2) Free Technical Assistance

- **Crop and fishery production:** soil management, seed selection, planting and irrigation methods, pests, fertilizers, harvesting and post-harvest, and field management
- **Soil analysis:** bring a dried soil sample (1 kilo)
- **Water sampling and analysis** for fish ponds
- **Pest and disease management**
- **Crop insurance enrollment** (Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation)
- **RSBSA enrollment** (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)
- **Business plan preparation** and marketing support for agri-fishery enterprises

Request these at your **district office (Municipal Agricultural Office)** or the main office.

---

## 3) For Fisherfolk

The CAgrO issues:

- **Fishing licenses** and **boat registration certificates** for municipal fishing vessels
- **Municipal fisheries licenses** for operators of fish cages, fish corrals, shallow fish shelters and seaweed farming
- **Auxiliary invoices** for fish and fishery products leaving Davao City (a fee of ₱200 per ton for fresh, frozen, dried or preserved fish; ₱30 per thousand bangus fry; ₱50 per thousand fingerlings or live fish)

The CAgrO has held onsite licensing and boat-registration drives in barangays together with the Department of Agriculture's RSBSA team and the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation. Ask your district office for the next schedule.
