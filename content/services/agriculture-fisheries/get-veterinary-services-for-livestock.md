# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock — Davao City

The **City Veterinarian's Office (CVO)**, through its Animal Husbandry and Disease Prevention and Control Division, serves farmers and pet owners in all barangays through its district offices.

---

## 1) Livestock Dispersal Program

Qualified farmers in Davao City can receive livestock to develop the industry and provide livelihood. The program covers animals such as chicken, goat, hog, cattle and carabao. Requirements:

1. Letter of intent (from an interested farmer, association or cooperative)
2. Barangay certification of residency
3. Two 1x1 pictures
4. Cedula (photocopy)
5. Certificate of training on livestock and poultry management and the dispersal policy (from the CVO)
6. Certificate of No Pending Obligation on any previous dispersal
7. Validation report from the district head supervisor

**Cooperatives** also submit a CDA/SEC registration certificate and a signed Board Resolution.

Submit the letter of intent to your **CVO district office**, which forwards it to the main office for consideration.

---

## 2) Artificial Insemination (Cattle and Carabao)

To upgrade the breed of your animals. For owners of female cattle and carabao in Davao City:

- A letter request
- Animal must be breedable (2½ to 3 years old, about 250 kilos), dewormed by the CVO, and in heat (about 18 hours)
- An animal chute in the area where AI is conducted
- A Unified National Artificial Insemination Program receipt and/or Philippine Carabao Center receipt

---

## 3) Dog and Cat Services

- **Free anti-rabies vaccination** for dogs and cats at least 3 months old and apparently healthy. Send a letter request, and keep the vaccination card for boosters. Available in all barangay districts.
- **Neutering** of dogs and cats 6 months and older, apparently healthy, with an anti-rabies vaccination at least 14 days before surgery. No food for 12 hours and no water for 6 hours before surgery.
- **Impounding:** stray animals are impounded and may be released to their owners after paying the fees.
