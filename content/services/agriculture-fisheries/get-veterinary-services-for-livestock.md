# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock — Davao City

The **City Veterinarian's Office (CVO)**, through its Animal Husbandry and Disease Prevention and Control Division, serves farmers and pet owners in all barangays through its district offices.

---

## 1) Livestock Dispersal Program

Qualified farmers in Davao City can receive livestock to develop the industry and provide livelihood. The program covers animals such as chicken, goat, hog, cattle and carabao. Requirements:

1. Letter of intent (from an interested farmer, association or cooperative)
2. Barangay certification of residency
3. Two 1x1 pictures
4. Cedula (photocopy)
5. Certificate of training on livestock and poultry management and the dispersal policy (from the CVO)
6. Certificate of No Pending Obligation on any previous dispersal
7. Validation report from the district head supervisor

**Cooperatives** also submit a CDA/SEC registration certificate and a signed Board Resolution.

**Steps**

1. Submit the letter of intent to your **CVO district office**, which forwards it to the main office and returns it to the district for validation.
2. Submit the required documents to the CVO, 2nd Floor, City Hall Annex Building, A. Pichon St. The CVO prepares an **Affidavit of Undertaking** for you to sign.
3. Have the affidavit notarized (the charter lists **₱500.00**) and return it within **1 working day**.
4. Claim the animal at the Animal Production Center concerned.

The charter lists a total of 4 days, 4 hours and 20 minutes.

---

## 2) Artificial Insemination

To upgrade the breed of your animals. For owners of female cattle and carabao in Davao City:

- A letter request
- Animal must be breedable (2½ to 3 years old, about 250 kilos), dewormed by the CVO, and in heat (about 18 hours)
- An animal chute in the area where AI is conducted
- A Unified National Artificial Insemination Program receipt and/or Philippine Carabao Center receipt

The charter also describes AI for **sows** (letter request, dewormed by the CVO, in heat, and a confirmation slip after AI). Ask your district office.

---

## 3) Dog and Cat Services

- **Free anti-rabies vaccination** for dogs and cats at least 3 months old and apparently healthy. Send a letter request, and keep the vaccination card for boosters. Available in all barangay districts.
- **Neutering** of **male** dogs and cats 6 months and older, apparently healthy and fit for surgery, with an anti-rabies vaccination at least 14 days before surgery. Send a letter request to your district office. No food for 12 hours and no water for 6 hours before surgery. Bring:
  - 1 roll of unused tissue paper
  - 7 pieces of 4x4 gauze pads
  - 1 piece of manila paper
  - 1 bottle of isopropyl or ethyl alcohol
  - 1 syringe per animal: 1cc for cats, 3cc for dogs
  - A signed waiver
- **Impounding:** stray animals are impounded at the **City Pound, Purok 3-A Cogon, Brgy. Malagos, Baguio District**. To claim an animal, get an order of payment at the City Pound, pay at the City Treasurer's Office at the Malagos Slaughterhouse or the Calinan District Hall, and return with the receipt. Impounded dogs are vaccinated against rabies before release. Fees:
  - Dogs: ₱500.00 for the first day and ₱50.00 per day for the next 2 days
  - Large animals: ₱1,000.00 and ₱100.00 per day until claimed
