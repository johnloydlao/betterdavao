# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting Activities — Davao City

The **City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)** supports community clean-ups, tree planting and environmental education. Its Natural Resources Operations Division handles reforestation and planting materials, and its Environmental and Waste Management Division handles waste and clean-ups.

---

## 1) Barangay Clean-up Drives

A 2023 memorandum of the City Mayor requires barangays to hold **regular clean-up drives** to address flooding. It also calls for stronger information drives on proper waste disposal, community participation in clean-ups, and a reporting system for garbage piling up near waterways. CENRO monitors the barangays' reports.

**How to take part**

- Ask your **Barangay Hall** when the next clean-up is, and join it.
- If your barangay or group is cleaning canals or waterways, **coordinate with CENRO** so the collected garbage can be hauled to the sanitary landfill.
- Households that litter or dump in public places can be given a citation ticket. See [Report illegal dumping](/report-illegal-dumping-or-waste-management-violations).

---

## 2) Tree Planting: Request Seedlings

CENRO provides **planting materials (seedlings) for watershed and mangrove areas**. Steps:

1. Submit a **request letter** with the attached requirements to CENRO's receiving section, **at least seven (7) days before** your activity.
2. If seedlings are available, CENRO prepares a withdrawal slip, which the division chief approves.
3. CENRO's technical staff do a **site visit and validation** with you, to check the planting site.
4. Take the signed **withdrawal slip** to CENRO, then go to the nursery, where the nursery workers release the seedlings.
5. **CENRO personnel assist your planting activity** and document it.

**Notes:** no tree planting is held on Sundays.

---

## 3) Environmental Awareness Lectures

Schools, groups and organizations can ask CENRO for an **environmental awareness lecture** by sending a request letter. The department head assigns a team, the office coordinates the schedule with you, and the lecture is given at your venue.
