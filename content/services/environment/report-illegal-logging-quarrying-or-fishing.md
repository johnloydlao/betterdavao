# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying or Fishing — Davao City

Illegal activities that harm natural resources can be reported through **Davao City Reports**, the city's official complaint platform, or to **CENRO**, which regulates quarrying and the city's natural resources.

---

## 1) Davao City Reports

Established in 2017, **Davao City Reports (DCR)** takes reports about illegal activity in Davao City by **text, call, email or Facebook message**. Reports are validated and forwarded to the concerned offices, which have **three to five days** to respond. You receive a **reference number** and a copy of the office's feedback on the action taken.

**Include:** the exact address, a landmark, the time and date of the incident, and photos taken on your phone.

- Web: [reports.davaocity.gov.ph](https://reports.davaocity.gov.ph/) (register, then click "Submit a Report"; updates are sent by email)
- Mobile: 0917 131 2333 or 0919 072 2222
- Email: [davaocityreports@davaocity.gov.ph](mailto:davaocityreports@davaocity.gov.ph)
- Facebook: Davao City Reports

---

## 2) Illegal Quarrying

Quarrying is regulated by the **City Mining Regulatory Board (CMRB)**, with CENRO as its secretariat. Commercial quarry permits are granted for sand and gravel (mainly along the Davao, Lasang and Matina rivers), earthfill and limestone.

- An **inter-agency task group** on quarrying inspects quarry sites, prepares violation reports and monitors the terms of quarry permits.
- CENRO runs **monitoring stations** that check quarry tickets on transported materials. In 2022 there were 30 stations with 80 personnel.
- CENRO's list of services includes the **apprehension of quarry violators**.

> **As reported, not yet on an official city page:** the task group was first created by Executive Order No. 48, s. 2020, with the CENRO head as chair. Edge Davao reported in October 2022 that Executive Order No. 54, s. 2022 formed an Inter-Agency Task Group for Quarrying Operations chaired by the Office of the City Mayor, with CENRO as a member. Confirm the current order with the office.

To report quarrying that looks unregulated or unpermitted, contact CENRO/CMRB staff at (082) 225-0744 or file a Davao City Reports complaint with the location and photos.

---

## 3) Illegal Logging and Illegal Fishing

The city's published pages do not describe a separate hotline or procedure for illegal logging or illegal fishing.

- **Illegal logging, or the cutting of trees:** CENRO is the city's environment and natural resources office. Report to CENRO or Davao City Reports.
- **Illegal fishing:** fishing licenses and municipal fisheries licenses are issued by the **City Agriculturist's Office** (its Fishery Resources Management Services Division). Report to Davao City Reports or to that office, and include the location and a photo.
