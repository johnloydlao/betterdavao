# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying or Fishing — Davao City

Illegal activities that harm natural resources can be reported through **Davao City Reports**, the city's official complaint platform, or to **CENRO**, which regulates quarrying and the city's natural resources.

---

## 1) Davao City Reports

Established in 2017, **Davao City Reports (DCR)** takes reports about illegal activity in Davao City by **text, call, email or Facebook message**. Reports are validated and forwarded to the concerned offices, which have **three to five days** to respond. You receive a **reference number** and a copy of the office's feedback on the action taken.

**Include:** the exact address, a landmark, the time and date of the incident, and photos or video taken on your phone.

- Email: davaocityreports@davaocity.gov.ph
- Facebook: Davao City Reports
- Mobile: (0919) 072 2222, or (0917) 131 2333 (calls and text). A 2024 city article gives the first number as 0917 142 2333, so confirm it.

---

## 2) Illegal Quarrying

Quarrying is regulated by the **City Mining Regulatory Board (CMRB)**, with CENRO as its secretariat. Commercial quarry permits are granted for sand and gravel, earthfill and limestone along the Davao, Lasang, Matina and Talomo rivers.

- An **inter-agency task group** (Executive Order No. 48, 2020), chaired by the CENRO head, inspects quarry sites, prepares violation reports and monitors the terms of quarry permits.
- CENRO runs **monitoring stations** that check quarry tickets on transported materials. In 2022 there were 30 stations with 80 personnel.
- CENRO's list of services includes the **apprehension of quarry violators**.

To report quarrying that looks unregulated or unpermitted, contact CENRO or file a Davao City Reports complaint with the location and photos.

---

## 3) Illegal Logging and Illegal Fishing

The city's published pages do not describe a separate hotline or procedure for illegal logging or illegal fishing.

- **Illegal logging, or the cutting of trees:** CENRO is the city's environment and natural resources office. Report to CENRO or Davao City Reports.
- **Illegal fishing:** fishing licenses and municipal fisheries licenses are issued by the **City Agriculturist's Office**, Fishery Resource Management Services. Report to Davao City Reports or to that office, and include the location and a photo.
