# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Davao City

The city-owned public markets are operated by the **City Economic Enterprise** under the City Administrator's Office. Stall rentals and other market fees are collected by the **City Treasurer's Office** at the market collection units, under the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao (Ordinance No. 0291-17): Sec. 348 (rates) and Sec. 347 (market classes, as amended by Ordinance No. 0148-19).

---

## 1) How to Get a Stall

Vacant stalls are leased through a **raffle**, per the 2024 Citizen's Charter (City Economic Enterprise, "Leasing of Market Stalls to the Public through Raffling"). It is open to **residents of Davao City**.

**Requirements:**

- Notarized application letter on the prescribed form (from the Market Supervisor)
- Birth certificate (1 original, 1 photocopy), from the PSA or the Local Civil Registrar
- Official receipt for the **₱400 application fee** (non-refundable), paid at a City Treasurer's Office district office
- Bank certificate as proof of financial capacity
- Two recent 2×2 ID pictures

**Steps:**

1. Watch for a **Notice of Vacancy**. It is posted for at least 10 days on the City Hall bulletin board, on the vacant stall, and in a visible place in the market.
2. Submit your application letter and requirements to pre-qualify for the raffle. The Market Committee validates the applicants and posts the names of qualified bidders.
3. Be present at the raffle of the vacant stall.
4. If you win, sign the contract.
5. Pay the **occupancy fee** (see below) using the Tax Order of Payment the Market Supervisor issues.
6. Occupy the stall.

The charter puts the total processing time at about 3 hours 55 minutes, not counting the posting period. Ask the **Market Supervisor** of the market you want, or the City Economic Enterprise, about current vacancies.

## 2) Market Classes

Fees depend on the class of the market, which is based on the average monthly income of the market during the preceding six months:

| Class | Average monthly income      |
| ----- | --------------------------- |
| A     | ₱2,000,000 or more          |
| B     | ₱1,500,000 up to ₱2,000,000 |
| C     | ₱700,000 up to ₱1,500,000   |
| D     | Less than ₱700,000          |

Ask the Market Master which class your market is.

---

> **These are base rates.** The figures below are the base rates in the 2017 Revenue Code (Sec. 348). The ordinance adds an automatic 15% increase effective 2018 and every four years after that, so the current amounts are higher. Get the exact figure from your Market Master or the City Economic Enterprise.

## 4) Stall Rental (per square meter, per day; billed monthly)

| Section                                                                                                                          | Class A | Class B | Class C | Class D |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| Meat                                                                                                                             | ₱40.00  | ₱28.50  | ₱17.00  | ₱13.50  |
| Dressed chicken and other poultry products                                                                                       | ₱36.00  | ₱26.50  | ₱16.00  | ₱13.00  |
| Fish                                                                                                                             | ₱38.00  | ₱25.50  | ₱15.50  | ₱12.00  |
| All other sections (vegetables and fruits, groceries, rice and corn, food court/eatery, dry goods, cold storage, advertisements) | ₱16.00  | ₱10.80  | ₱6.80   | ₱5.40   |

For example, a 4 m² Class A vegetable stall at the base rate is ₱16 × 4 m² × 30 days ≈ **₱1,920 a month**.

Better-located stalls pay an **added percentage**: front corner stalls 20%, front stalls 15%, inside corner stalls 10%.

**Transient or ambulant vendors** pay per square meter per day: ₱8.00 (Class A), ₱6.00 (B), ₱4.00 (C) and ₱2.00 (D).

---

## 5) How and When to Pay

1. Get the **Tax Order of Payment** from the Economic Enterprise Unit of the Market Master.
2. Get a priority number from the Officer of the Day at the City Treasurer's Office collection unit, then pay at window 2 of the CTO District Office or the market's collection unit. The 2024 Citizen's Charter puts processing at about 13 minutes.
3. Keep your official receipt.

**Monthly rentals are due within the first 20 days of the month.** Late payment carries a **25% surcharge** plus **2% interest per month**, up to 36 months.

Market certifications and clearances for stall holders are also issued by the Treasurer's Office (Bankerohan Public Market collection unit), with a ₱50 secretary's fee listed in the 2024 Citizen's Charter.
