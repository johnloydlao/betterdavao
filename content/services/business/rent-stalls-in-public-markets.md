# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — Davao City

The city-owned public markets are operated by the **City Economic Enterprise** under the City Administrator's Office. Stall rentals and other market fees are collected by the **City Treasurer's Office** at the market collection units, under the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao (Ordinance No. 0291-17, Section 348), as amended by Ordinance No. 0148-19.

---

## 1) How to Get a Stall

The Citizen's Charter and the city's pages do not describe how stalls are awarded or what the application requirements are. Ask the **City Economic Enterprise** or the **Market Master** of the market you want about vacant stalls and how to apply. Awardees pay an **occupancy fee** (see below).

---

## 2) Market Classes

Fees depend on the class of the market, which is based on the average monthly income of the market during the preceding six months:

| Class | Average monthly income      |
| ----- | --------------------------- |
| A     | ₱2,000,000 or more          |
| B     | ₱1,500,000 up to ₱2,000,000 |
| C     | ₱700,000 up to ₱1,500,000   |
| D     | Less than ₱700,000          |

Ask the Market Master which class your market is.

## 4) Monthly Rental

Stall rentals are charged **per square meter per day**, by market class and type of stall. In most sections other than meat, the rate is **₱16.00 (Class A), ₱10.80 (B), ₱6.80 (C) and ₱5.40 (D)**. Meat stalls pay higher rates.

Better-located stalls pay an **added percentage**: front corner stalls 20%, front stalls 15%, inside corner stalls 10%.

The ordinance provides for an **automatic 15% increase every four years** starting in 2018, so current rates may be higher than the figures above. Confirm the rate with the Market Master.

**Transient or ambulant vendors** pay per square meter per day: ₱8.00 (Class A), ₱6.00 (B), ₱4.00 (C) and ₱2.00 (D).

---

## 5) How and When to Pay

1. Get the **Tax Order of Payment** from the Economic Enterprise Unit of the Market Master.
2. Get a priority number from the Officer of the Day at the City Treasurer's Office collection unit, then pay at window 2 of the CTO District Office or the market's collection unit. Processing takes about 13 minutes.
3. Keep your official receipt.

**Monthly rentals are due within the first 20 days of the month.** Late payment carries a **25% surcharge** plus **2% interest per month**, up to 36 months.

Market certifications and clearances for stall holders are also issued by the Treasurer's Office (Bankerohan Public Market collection unit), with a ₱50 secretary's fee listed in the charter.
