# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos or Tourism Promotions — Davao City

The City Government of Davao promotes local products and tourism through the **City Tourism Operations Office** and the city's tourism website, [tourism.davaocity.gov.ph](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/), which has sections on Events, Services and **M.I.C.E.** (meetings, incentives, conventions and exhibitions).

---

## How to Find Out About Opportunities

1. Check the **Events** and **M.I.C.E.** sections of the [Davao City tourism website](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/).
2. Ask the **City Tourism Operations Office** about upcoming trade fairs, expos and tourism promotions, and how local businesses can take part.
3. Make sure your **Mayor's Permit** is current. See [Renew permits](/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes).

No citywide calendar of trade fairs or application procedure is published on the city sites I reviewed, so the office is the best place to ask.
