# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges or Drainage — Davao City

The **City Engineer's Office (CEO)** accepts requests, inquiries and complaints on engineering matters, such as a request to **inspect a road** or a complaint about a **drainage problem**. Reports can also go to **Davao City Reports**, the city's official complaint platform.

---

## 1) Report to the City Engineer's Office

1. Prepare a **letter request or complaint** with your **contact number** (2 originals and 1 photocopy), and a **sketch plan** of the location if you can. The charter says to submit 3 copies of the letter with any supporting documents.
2. Submit it to the City Engineer's Office. A clerk evaluates and records the letter, and the Officer-in-Charge decides the course of action.
3. The concerned division or section acts on it. The charter lists **no fee** and up to **15 days** for the division or section to act.

---

## 2) Report to Davao City Reports

You can also file a report by **text, call, email or Facebook message**. The concerned office has three to five days to respond, and you receive a reference number. Include the exact address, a landmark, the date and time, and photos.

- Email: davaocityreports@davaocity.gov.ph
- Mobile: (0919) 072 2222 or (0917) 131 2333 (calls and text)

---

## 3) Related Services

- **Drainage clearance** for owners, contractors and developers who build or develop land (a regulatory permit, not a repair request).
- **Excavation permits** for digging up roads.
- **Road right-of-way certification.**
- For **traffic signs, pedestrian lanes or railings**, the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) takes requests.
- For **flooding and drainage clogged by garbage**, see [Report illegal dumping](/report-illegal-dumping-or-waste-management-violations).

The charter does not list a separate process for reporting damaged bridges. Use the City Engineer's Office or Davao City Reports, and describe the damage and location.
