# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems — Davao City

Water service in Davao City is provided mainly by the **Davao City Water District (DCWD)**, which is a separate agency from the City Government. The City Government's Citizen's Charter does not describe a city-run process for connecting to a barangay water system.

---

## 1) Apply for a DCWD Service Connection

DCWD handles new service connections (NSC). Its official website is [davao-water.gov.ph](https://www.davao-water.gov.ph/). DCWD's own requirements and fees could not be read for this guide, so the details below come from news reports.

> **As reported in the news. Confirm with DCWD.** These points come from news articles from 2024 and January 2025, not from DCWD's own published rules. Fees, requirements and promotions may have changed.
>
> - You can apply online through DCWD's website or in person at DCWD's offices in **Matina, Bajada or Toril** ([Edge Davao, May 2024](https://edgedavao.net/competitive-edge/2024/05/dcwd-extends-easy-installment-for-new-service-connections/)).
> - For a face-to-face application, bring a **valid ID of the account holder** and **proof of possession of the property** ([SunStar Davao, March 2024](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/dcwds-easy-installment-option-for-water-connection-applicants-up-until-mar-31)).
> - The new service connection fee is **₱6,000**. DCWD has offered an **installment option** for residential connections with a ½-inch meter: ₱500 up front, and the rest at ₱500 a month for 11 months on the water bill, with no interest. It ran in 2024 ([Edge Davao, March 2024](https://edgedavao.net/competitive-edge/2024/03/affordable-payment-option-to-get-a-dcwd-water-service-connection/)) and was renewed in January 2025 ([Edge Davao, January 2025](https://edgedavao.net/latest-news/2025/01/dcwd-projects-to-cater-more-customers-in-2025)). Ask DCWD whether it is still open.
> - You sign the **service connection contract** only after all documentary requirements are satisfied and your plumbing provision (_abang_) is approved.
> - DCWD had suspended new service connections in several areas of District 2 and lifted that suspension in early 2024 (SunStar Davao, March 2024). Ask DCWD whether new connections are open in your area.

## 2) City Help for Housing Beneficiaries

Beneficiaries of the city's relocation and resettlement projects can get a certification from the **Davao City Housing Office (DCHO)** for a **water connection** (required by DCWD) and for building and electrical permits (required by Davao Light and Power Company).

- The DCHO checks your name against its master list of beneficiaries and issues an order of payment. If you are not a housing beneficiary, you submit a **barangay certification** when you request.
- Pay the **₱50 certification fee** at the City Treasurer's Office window, then show the receipt at the Housing Division.
- You get a claim stub. The charter says the certification is claimed after about **3 days**.

See [Apply for socialized housing](/services/housing-land-use/apply-for-socialized-housing-or-relocation-projects) for the Housing Office's other services.

---

## 3) Barangay Water Systems

The City Government's published sources do not describe how a barangay or community water association is formed or how residents join one. Ask your **Barangay Hall** whether your barangay has its own water system, and contact **DCWD** about your area.
