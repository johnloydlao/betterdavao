# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems — Davao City

Water service in Davao City is provided mainly by the **Davao City Water District (DCWD)**, which is a separate agency from the City Government. The City Government's Citizen's Charter does not describe a city-run process for connecting to a barangay water system.

---

## 1) Apply for a DCWD Service Connection

News reports on DCWD say that:

- Applicants attend an **orientation**, which is the first step in getting a water service connection.
- The new service connection fee is **₱6,000**. DCWD has offered an **installment option**, with ₱500 paid up front and the balance spread over 11 months (offered in 2024 and 2025, so confirm it is still open).
- You sign the **service connection contract** after satisfying the documents and after your plumbing provision (_abang_) is approved.
- New connection applications are accepted in an increasing number of barangays. DCWD has had to limit new connections in some areas.
- Subdivision developers send a request letter to DCWD, which provides the checklist.

DCWD's website and offices have the document checklist. The City Government does not publish it.

---

## 2) City Help for Housing Beneficiaries

Beneficiaries of the city's relocation and resettlement projects can get a certification from the **Davao City Housing Office** for a **water connection** (for the Davao City Water District) and for building and electrical permits (for Davao Light and Power Company). See [Apply for socialized housing](/apply-for-socialized-housing-or-relocation-projects).

---

## 3) Barangay Water Systems

The sources I reviewed do not describe how a barangay or community water association is formed or how residents join one. Ask your **Barangay Hall** whether your barangay has its own water system, and contact **DCWD** about your area.

---

_Sources: news reports on DCWD service connections (Edge Davao and SunStar Davao, 2024–2025); City Government of Davao Citizen's Charter, Volume II (2024 revision), Davao City Housing Office. DCWD is not part of the City Government, and DCWD's own pages were not reviewed._
