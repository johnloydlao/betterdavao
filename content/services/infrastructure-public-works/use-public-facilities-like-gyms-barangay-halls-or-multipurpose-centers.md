# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls or Multipurpose Centers — Davao City

The City Government manages several public facilities, and some can be booked or borrowed.

---

## 1) Davao City Recreation Center (Almendras Gym)

The **City Economic Enterprise (CEE)** leases the **Davao City Recreation Center (DCRC)**, also known as Almendras Gym, at Quezon Boulevard, Barangay 76-A, as a short-term venue for events. Use is either paid, or free of charge with the approval of the City Mayor or an authorized representative (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, pp. 386-388).

**Requirements:**

- A **request letter or form** addressed to the City Mayor, with your details, the event time frame, the purpose and other specifications. You can write the letter in your own format, or get a request form from the DCRC Office.
- For **free use**: approval from the City Mayor (from the City Mayor's Office or the City Administrator's Office).
- For **paid use**: an **Order of Payment** from the DCRC Office, and the **Official Receipt** from the City Treasurer's Office.

**Steps:**

1. Present the request letter or form at the DCRC. The officer-in-charge checks the details, the schedule, and the amenities and equipment available, then tells you the status.
2. Confirm the booking.
3. Pay at the City Treasurer's Office using the order of payment.
4. Present the official receipt to the DCRC booking officer to finalize the booking.
5. For **free use**, write a letter request to the City Mayor or the City Administrator through the City Economic Enterprise Manager. The City Mayor's Office or the City Administrator approves or disapproves it, and the DCRC head is informed.

The charter lists about 1 hour and 45 minutes in total.

**Fees** (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, Table 1, p. 388):

## 2) Sound System, Tent or Chairs

The **City General Services Office (CGSO)** provides a **sound system with operators**, and lends **tents or chairs**, for activities (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, p. 468).

- **Who can request:** barangay councils, schools, NGOs and government offices.
- **How:** sign the client log book in the office lobby and submit a **letter request with your contact number** (1 original per request). The office checks that the items are available and free of prior commitments, **calendars your activity**, and tells you whether the request is approved or denied.
- The charter lists **no fee**, and about 21 minutes of processing at the office.

---

## 3) Barangay Halls and Other Centers

The charter does not list a city process for using a barangay hall, a multipurpose center or a barangay gym. These facilities are managed by the barangay. Ask your **Barangay Hall** how to reserve one.
