# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls or Multipurpose Centers — Davao City

The City Government manages several public facilities, and some can be booked or borrowed.

---

## 1) Davao City Recreation Center (Almendras Gym)

The **City Economic Enterprise (CEE)** takes **bookings for the use of the Davao City Recreation Center (Almendras Gym)**.

The city's published pages I reviewed do not list the booking requirements, fees or forms. Contact the City Economic Enterprise, or visit the Recreation Center, to ask about availability and what to bring.

---

## 2) Sound System, Tent or Chairs

The **City General Services Office** provides a **sound system with operators**, and lends **tents or chairs**, for activities.

- **Who can request:** barangay councils, schools, NGOs and government offices.
- **How:** submit a **letter request with your contact number** (1 original per request) at the City General Services Office. The office checks that the items are available and free of prior commitments, and **calendars your activity**.
- The charter lists **no fee**, and about 10 minutes of processing at the office.

---

## 3) Barangay Halls and Other Centers

The charter does not list a city process for using a barangay hall, a multipurpose center or a barangay gym. These facilities are managed by the barangay. Ask your **Barangay Hall** how to reserve one.
