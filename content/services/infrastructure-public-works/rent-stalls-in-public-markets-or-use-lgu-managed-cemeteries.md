# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries — Davao City

Both services go through the **City Economic Enterprise (CEE)**, which handles leasing in the city-owned public markets and applications for grave lots, niches and ossuaries in the **public cemeteries**. The **City Treasurer's Office** collects the fees.

---

## 1) Market Stalls

CEE leases stalls to the public in the city-owned public markets. See [Rent stalls in public markets](/services/business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets) for the raffle process, occupancy fees, monthly rental rates by market class, and payment rules.

---

## 2) Public Cemeteries

CEE Cemetery Operations accepts applications for **grave lots, compartmentalized niches and ossuaries**, and for the accommodation of a **pauper's burial** in the city-owned public cemeteries. The fees below are from the 2024 Citizen's Charter. Confirm the current amounts with CEE Cemetery Operations.

### Lease of a Grave Lot

**Requirements** (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, pp. 397-398):

- Notarized application letter and Contract to Lease (prescribed form), 2 originals, from Cemetery Operations, City Economic Enterprise Office (City Hall)
- Death certificate, 1 photocopy, from the Local Civil Registrar
- Tax Order of Payment payable to the City Treasurer's Office, 1 original, from Cemetery Operations
- Official Receipt of payment, 1 original, from the City Treasurer's Central Office

**Steps:**

1. Apply for a permit to lease a grave lot. You receive the application form and the contract of lease form.
2. Get the **Tax Order of Payment** from Cemetery Operations.
3. Pay at the **City Treasurer's Central Office**: **₱2,200 (adult)** or **₱1,100 (child)**.
4. Have the contract of lease notarized by a notary public or the City Legal Office. The notarial fee depends on the lawyer.
5. Cemetery Operations reviews the contract for signature by the City Economic Enterprise Manager.
6. Show the receipt and the approved contract to the cemetery caretaker, who schedules the burial.

The charter lists about 1 hour and 10 minutes in total.

### Compartmentalized Niches and Ossuaries (Wireless Public Cemetery)

Niches and ossuaries are leased at the **Wireless Public Cemetery**. The requirements and steps are the same as for a grave lot. At the last step, you present the approved contract to the Officer-in-Charge of the Operation of Cemeteries at the Wireless Public Cemetery (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, pp. 399-400).

| Compartmentalized niches and ossuaries | Fee              |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Niche, 1st level                       | ₱7,040           |
| Niche, 2nd level                       | ₱8,800           |
| Niche, 3rd level                       | ₱7,920           |
| Niche, 4th level                       | ₱6,160           |
| Ossuary                                | ₱220.00 per year |

### Pauper's Burial (Ma-a Public Cemetery)

This is burial assistance for **indigent residents of Davao City**. There is **no fee**, and the burial takes place at the **Ma-a Public Cemetery** (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. I, pp. 395-396).

**Requirements:**

- Death certificate duly certified by the Local Civil Registrar and stamped by the Social Security System as a non-member, 1 photocopy
- Barangay Certificate of Indigency, 1 original and 1 photocopy, from the barangay where the deceased or the family resides
- Certificate of Low Income, 1 original and 1 photocopy, from the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)

**Steps:**

1. Submit the requirements.
2. With help from the funeral parlor, present the documents to the cemetery caretaker at Ma-a Cemetery.
3. The interment or burial is scheduled at the Ma-a Public Cemetery.
4. The funeral parlor processes the documents for the City Government to pay for the pauper's burial.

## 3) Cemetery Fees in the Revenue Code

The City Treasurer's Office collects cemetery charges under **Section 317 of the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao** (Ordinance No. 0291-17). The rental of a city cemetery lot runs for **six years** (2024 Citizen's Charter, Vol. II, Annex 17, pp. 201-202).

**A. Rehabilitated and newly established public cemetery**

| Rental fee                      | Fee     |
| ------------------------------- | ------- |
| Grave lot, adult section        | ₱13,200 |
| Grave lot, children section     | ₱5,500  |
| Compartmentalized niche, ground | ₱7,040  |
| Compartmentalized niche, second | ₱8,800  |
| Compartmentalized niche, third  | ₱7,920  |
| Compartmentalized niche, fourth | ₱6,160  |

No physical improvements are allowed on the lots, niches and ossuaries.

**B. Pre-rehabilitated public cemetery**

| Charge                                                                                                                  | Fee    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Burial fee for grave lots, niche or pantheon, adult section                                                             | ₱2,200 |
| Burial fee for grave lots, niche or pantheon, children section                                                          | ₱1,100 |
| Annual renewal fee where the lease contract has expired: grave lot, adult section                                       | ₱275   |
| Annual renewal fee where the lease contract has expired: grave lot, children section                                    | ₱220   |
| Annual renewal fee where the lease contract has expired: niches (niches or pantheon placed on top, or _patong_ burials) | ₱275   |
| Burial without lease contract from 2005 and below: grave lot, adult section                                             | ₱2,200 |
| Burial without lease contract from 2005 and below: grave lot, children section                                          | ₱1,100 |
| Burial without lease contract from 2005 and below: niches (niches or pantheon placed on top, or _patong_ burials)       | ₱2,200 |
| Exhumation of remains, per set of bones                                                                                 | ₱500   |

The charter does not say which public cemeteries are "rehabilitated or newly established" and which are "pre-rehabilitated". Ask **CEE Cemetery Operations** which fee category applies to your cemetery.

**Where to pay:** the CEE steps above say to pay at the **City Treasurer's Central Office**. The City Treasurer's Office section of the charter lists **windows 9 and 10 in the Sangguniang Panlungsod lobby**, or an assigned collector at a CTO district office, with about 3 minutes of processing. Bring the Tax Order of Payment from CEE.
