# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries — Davao City

Both services go through the **City Economic Enterprise (CEE)**, which handles leasing in the city-owned public markets and applications for grave lots, niches and ossuaries in the **public cemeteries**. The **City Treasurer's Office** collects the fees.

---

## 1) Market Stalls

CEE leases stalls to the public in the city-owned public markets. See [Rent stalls in public markets](/rent-stalls-in-public-markets) for the occupancy fees, monthly rental rates by market class, and payment rules.

---

## 2) Public Cemeteries

CEE accepts applications for **grave lots, compartmentalized niches and ossuaries**, and for the accommodation of a **pauper's burial** in the city-owned public cemeteries.

**How it works:**

1. Apply at the **City Economic Enterprise** and get a **Tax Order of Payment**.
2. Pay at **windows 9 and 10 in the Sangguniang Panlungsod lobby**, or at a City Treasurer's district office. The charter lists about 3 minutes.

**Fees** (2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao, Section 317). Rental of a city cemetery lot runs for **six years**:

| Rehabilitated and newly established public cemetery | Fee     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Grave lot, adult section                            | ₱13,200 |
| Grave lot, children section                         | ₱5,500  |
| Niche, ground level                                 | ₱7,040  |
| Niche, second level                                 | ₱8,800  |
| Niche, third level                                  | ₱7,920  |
| Niche, fourth level                                 | ₱6,160  |

No physical improvements are allowed on the lots, niches and ossuaries.

In the **pre-rehabilitated public cemeteries**, the burial fee for a grave lot, niche or pantheon is **₱2,200 (adult)** or **₱1,100 (children)**. Annual renewal fees for older burials (2005 and below) are ₱2,200 for adult grave lots and niches and ₱1,100 for children, and **exhumation** costs ₱500 per set of bones.

The charter does not list the documents needed for a cemetery application. Ask the City Economic Enterprise.
