# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems — Davao City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** publishes advisories and runs the city's early warning systems.

---

## 1) Where to Get Alerts and Information

- **CDRRMO Facebook page:** [facebook.com/DavaoDRRMO](https://www.facebook.com/DavaoDRRMO) posts **real-time weather advisories**.
- **Davao City Disaster Radio (DCDR), 87.5 FM:** the city-owned radio station used for CDRRMO announcements and for disaster-related interviews with city officials. Phone (082) 298 8781.
- **Your barangay:** barangays relay warnings and calls for evacuation to residents.
- **CDRRMO office page:** [CDRRMO on davaocity.gov.ph](https://www.davaocity.gov.ph/departments/social-services/cmo-disaster-council/), with the office's address, phone numbers and email.

---

## 2) Early Warning Systems

- **Sirens:** the city has installed sirens in the barangays. After the first citywide drill in 2017, when the city had only six or seven sirens, the CDRRMO increased the number. Barangays also sound sirens to start a pre-emptive evacuation for river overflow.
- **Coastal cameras:** the CDRRMO monitors the sea with coastal cameras to watch for a tsunami, such as receding sea water, so no one has to wait and watch.
- **Communications van:** the city has procured a communications vehicle with a hydraulic tower and antenna, so the CDRRMO can keep communicating even when it operates from safe zones far from its usual base.
- **Drills:** the annual Shake Out drill and the Riverwide Simex practice these systems. See [Join disaster drills](/services/disaster-preparedness/join-disaster-drills-and-evacuation-planning).

---

## 3) What to Do When You Hear a Warning

- Do not panic. Follow your barangay's instructions and call for evacuation.
- **Earthquake:** duck, cover and hold, then move to your safe zone.
- **Tsunami or river overflow:** evacuate to your barangay's safe zone or evacuation center.

---

## 4) Prepare Ahead

- Ask your Barangay Hall for your **safe zone** and evacuation center.
- Save the emergency numbers in [Emergency response procedures](/services/disaster-preparedness/learn-about-emergency-response-procedures-and-evacuation-routes), or see the site's [hotlines page](/hotlines).
- Join your barangay's drills.
- Schools and other groups can request training, drills, seminars or workshops from the CDRRMO. See [How to join](/services/disaster-preparedness/join-disaster-drills-and-evacuation-planning).

The city's published pages do not describe a mobile-phone text alert service, so rely on the sources above.
