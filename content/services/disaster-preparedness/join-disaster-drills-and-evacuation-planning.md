# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning — Davao City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** runs the city's drills and trains barangays, schools, establishments and volunteers. Its services include raising public awareness through **drills and exercises, especially at the barangay and community level**.

---

## 1) Citywide Drills

- **Earthquake and Tsunami "Shake Out" Drill:** held citywide, with sirens in the barangays. The 5th drill, on July 25, 2025, drew about **30,000 participants** from schools, barangays and establishments. In the 2024 drill, a first siren sounded at 9:00 AM for the earthquake and a second at 9:05 for the tsunami. Residents were told to **duck, cover and hold** and, for the tsunami, to follow the instructions of officials and leaders for a safe evacuation.
- **Riverwide Simulation Exercise (Simex):** trains communities for river overflow and tests the responders. The third one, on September 27, 2025, covered barangays near the Davao River: Mandug, Waan, Ma-a, Tigatto, 9-B, 9-A, 10-A, 5-A, 8-A, 2-A, 1-A, Matina Crossing and Bucana. A previous Simex was held in Barangay Toril.

Shops, malls, hospitals, schools, condominiums, national government offices and barangays all take part, and join preparatory meetings with the CDRRMO before each drill.

---

## 2) How to Join

1. **Residents:** watch for the CDRRMO's drill announcements (see the Facebook page below) and your Barangay Hall's call to take part. Barangay leaders coordinate their communities.
2. **Schools, businesses and organizations:** contact the CDRRMO to ask about joining the next drill or requesting a preparedness activity.
3. **Volunteers:** the CDRRMO coordinates the deployment of community responders and organizes response clusters. Ask the office how to get involved.

---

## 3) Evacuation Planning

- Barangay leaders take part in mapping **safe zones**. After Phivolcs issued an updated tsunami map in 2024, more barangays were added, including Obrero and Ma-a, and some earlier safe zones were reviewed.
- Ask your **Barangay Hall** where your barangay's safe zone and evacuation center are, and the route to reach them. The city has not published a citywide list of evacuation centers or routes.
