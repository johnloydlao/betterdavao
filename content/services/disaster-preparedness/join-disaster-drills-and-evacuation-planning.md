# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning — Davao City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** runs the city's drills and trains barangays, schools, establishments and volunteers. Its services include raising public awareness through **drills and exercises, especially at the barangay and community level**.

---

## 1) Citywide Drills

- **Earthquake and Tsunami "Shake Out" Drill:** held citywide every year, with sirens in the barangays. The 5th drill, on July 25, 2025, drew about **30,000 participants** from schools, barangays and establishments. In the 2024 drill, a first siren sounded at 9:00 AM for the earthquake and a second at 9:05 for the tsunami. Residents were told to **duck, cover and hold** and, for the tsunami, to follow the instructions of officials and leaders for a safe evacuation.
- **Riverwide Simulation Exercise (Simex):** trains communities for river overflow and tests the responders. For the third one, on September 27, 2025, the city announced it would cover barangays near the Davao River: Mandug, Waan, Ma-a, Tigatto, 9-B, 9-A, 10-A, 5-A, 8-A, 2-A, 1-A, Matina Crossing and Bucana. A previous Simex was held in Barangay Toril.

For the 2024 drill, malls, hospitals, schools, establishments, national government offices, condominiums and barangays attended preparatory meetings with the CDRRMO.

In May 2026, the CDRRMO said the next citywide shake out and tsunami Simex would be held in July. Dates change each year, so watch the CDRRMO Facebook page for announcements.

---

## 2) How to Join

1. **Residents:** watch for the CDRRMO's drill announcements (see the Facebook page below) and your Barangay Hall's call to take part. Barangay leaders coordinate their communities.
2. **Schools, businesses and organizations:** the CDRRMO provides training, drills, seminars and workshops on request. Anyone may request one.
   - Send a **request letter addressed to the CDRRM Officer** (one original), by walk-in, email or other means.
   - The CDRRMO approves the request and assigns technical staff to coordinate with you.
   - If you have not heard from the CDRRMO, follow up on your request.
   - There is **no fee**.
3. **Volunteers:** the CDRRMO coordinates the deployment of community responders and organizes response clusters. Ask the office how to get involved.

---

## 3) Evacuation Planning

- Barangay leaders take part in mapping **safe zones**. After Phivolcs issued an updated tsunami map in 2024, more barangays were included, not only coastal ones: the CDRRMO named Obrero, Ma-a and some barangays in Poblacion. Some earlier safe zones were found to be in impact areas and are no longer safe zones.
- Ask your **Barangay Hall** whether your barangay is affected, where its safe zone and evacuation center are, and the route to reach them. The city has not published a citywide list of evacuation centers or routes.

> **As reported (SunStar Davao, October 23, 2019):** the CDRRMO has posted barangay evacuation maps on its Facebook page. Those maps predate the 2024 tsunami map update, so check the Facebook page for the latest and confirm with the office or your Barangay Hall.
