# Free Health Services

The Davao City Government offers free **check-ups, medicines, and vaccines** through the City Health Office (CHO), barangay health centers, and partner programs.

---

## 1. Free Medical Check-ups

| Service              | Where                        | Schedule                                     | Requirements                                              | More Info                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General consultation | City/Barangay Health Centers | Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM (varies by health center) | Individual Treatment Record (ITR) — issued on first visit | [Learn More](https://davaocity.gov.ph/departments/social-services/city-health-office/) |
