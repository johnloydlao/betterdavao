# Go to the Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — Davao City

For basic and preventive care, start at your barangay **health center** or the **City Health Office (CHO)**. For hospital treatment or confinement, one of the main public hospitals in Davao City is the **Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC)**, operated under the Department of Health. The CHO also runs two **district hospitals**.

> **In an emergency, call 911** or go straight to the nearest emergency room. See the [Hotlines page](/hotlines) for other emergency numbers.

## 2) City-Run District Hospitals (Marilog and Paquibato)

The 2024 Citizen's Charter lists three hospital services run by the CHO at **Marilog District Hospital** and **Paquibato District Hospital**. For all three, the charter's checklist of requirements is "None", and you pay the hospital's posted rates or charges.

- **Outpatient Service:** "Medical care provided on an outpatient basis, including diagnosis, observation, consultation, treatment, intervention, and rehabilitation services." Laboratory, ECG and radiology are available as ancillary services. Registration is "First come first serve basis". Fees: "Refer to posted rates/fees".
- **Patient Admission Service:** "24 hours operation daily". Patients are admitted through the Emergency Room. Fees: "Refer to Hospital Charges", settled at the cashier on discharge.
- **Accident Emergency Service:** "24 hours operation daily", for "cardiac arrests, serious illnesses, trauma, Vehicular Accidents, Stabbing, Mauling, Gunshot and other Emergency Cases that needs immediate attention without any delay". Patients who need a higher-level facility are referred after initial management. For obstetric emergencies, "Only imminent deliveries will be catered". Fees: "Hospital Charges".

The charter does not give the hospitals' addresses or phone numbers. Ask the CHO for directions and contact details.

## 3) Before You Go

- Bring a **valid ID**.
- Bring your **PhilHealth** details if you have them, and ask the hospital which documents it requires.
- If you are unsure where to go, the CHO can provide **counseling and referral**.

## 4) Help With Hospital Bills: Lingap para sa Mahirap

The Mayor's **Lingap para sa Mahirap Program** gives medical assistance to Davao City residents in a crisis situation. It issues a **Guarantee Letter** (with a Certificate of Eligibility and Certificate of Indigency) for hospital bills, dialysis, procedures and laboratories, medicine, and funeral expenses. There is **no fee**, and the charter lists about **19 minutes** of processing at the office.

**Proof of residency** (any one):

- a barangay certificate indicating _Lingap Assistance_ as the purpose, or a voter's certificate (accepted for every type of assistance)
- a **government-issued ID** (photocopy), also accepted for a government hospital and for procedures, apparatus or laboratories
- a **valid ID** (photocopy), also accepted for medicine

**What else to bring** (by type of assistance):

- **Hospital (private):** final and updated hospital bill, and a medical certificate, certificate of confinement or clinical summary. If the patient is already discharged, add a promissory note from the hospital billing section.
- **Hospital (government):** final hospital bill (3 originals)
- **Dialysis:** price quotation and a medical abstract from the dialysis center (original for first-timers, photocopy for old clients)
- **Procedures, apparatus or laboratories:** price quotation and a medical certificate, medical abstract or clinical summary
- **Medicine:** a "Medicine prescription (government-issued)", from a government hospital or clinic or the City Health Office, and a "Medical Certificate (for Epoetin and Albumin)"
- **Funeral:** funeral contract (funeral bill) and a certified true copy of the death certificate. If the deceased is already buried, add a promissory note from the funeral parlor.
- **Accidents or criminal incidents:** a police report or traffic incident report (for hospital and procedure assistance). For funeral assistance after a vehicular accident, bring a certified true copy of the police or traffic incident report.

**Where:** Lingap para sa Mahirap Program, DMC Compound, J.P. Laurel Ave., Davao City. Phone: (082) 233-4961. SPMC's About Us page says SPMC "began as the _Davao Medical Center_" (DMC).

The charter does not state the amount of assistance. Ask the office.

For other kinds of crisis aid, see [Receive relief goods or cash aid during disasters](/services/social-welfare/receive-relief-goods-or-cash-aid-during-disasters).
