# Join Health Programs (Nutrition, Dengue Control, TB Treatment) — Davao City

The **City Health Office (CHO)** runs disease-prevention and treatment programs through its main office and the city's health centers.

## 2) Nutrition Services (Dietary Counseling and Medical Nutrition Therapy)

The City Government of Davao Citizen's Charter (2024) lists this service under the CHO Nutrition Division.

- **Who may avail:** "Patients having Cardiovascular diseases, Tuberculosis, AIDS, patients referred by SPMC and other government/private hospital upon discharge that needs diet modification/diet plan" and "Parents/Guardian of malnourished children".
- **What to bring:** an MNT Kardex Form or Individual Treatment Record Form (1 original), which you get from the CHO Nutrition Division.
- **Fee:** none. The charter lists about **25 minutes** of processing.

**Steps:**

1. Go to the Nutrition Division for counseling and say why you need medical nutrition therapy.
2. Fill out the MNT Kardex Form or Individual Treatment Record Form. Returning clients have their record pulled from the file.
3. The Nutrition Officer assesses your condition and prepares a **diet plan**, including your daily calorie needs and which foods to avoid and which are allowed.
4. Come back for follow-up if the officer sets a schedule.

## 3) HIV Counseling and Testing (VCT)

The charter describes this as "Free Voluntary Counseling and Testing (VCT) Services" at the CHO **Reproductive Health and Wellness Center**.

- **Who may avail:** all
- **What to bring:** no requirements

**Steps:**

1. Ask for HIV testing and get the VCT forms.
2. Return the completed forms. A counselor talks with you before the test, and your blood is taken for testing.
3. Get your result with post-test counseling.
4. If the result is reactive, a case manager links you to care.

For the link-to-care step, the charter lists laboratory fees "If RHWC labs": **₱1,510.00** for complete blood chemistry with CBC and **₱2,200.00** for an initial CD4 test without PhilHealth, a total of **₱3,710.00**. Ask the center which of these apply to you.

---

## 4) TB, Dengue and Other Programs

Of the programs above, only **nutrition services** and **HIV counseling and testing** have published steps in the 2024 Citizen's Charter. The charter does not describe enrollment steps for TB treatment or dengue control, so ask your health center or the CHO.

---

## 5) Community Health Education

The CHO runs **Purok Kalusugan**, a program of lectures and education campaigns in puroks and barangays, and works with schools to bring health personnel on campus.

---

## 6) How to Join

1. Visit your barangay's health center, or the CHO main office on Magallanes Street.
2. Tell the staff which program you need, such as TB treatment, dengue concerns or nutrition.
3. For programs not described above, ask about eligibility, schedules and what to bring.
